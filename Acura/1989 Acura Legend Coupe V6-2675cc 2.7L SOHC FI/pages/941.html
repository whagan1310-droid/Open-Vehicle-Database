Bleeding Hydraulic System

Use only DOT 3 or 4 brake fluid.
1. Attach suitable hose to clutch bleeder screw.
2. Position hose in suitable container.
3. Fill clutch master cylinder.
4. Slowly pump clutch pedal until no bubbles appear at bleeder hose.
5. Refill clutch master cylinder.
6. Torque bleeder screw to 8 ft. lbs.
