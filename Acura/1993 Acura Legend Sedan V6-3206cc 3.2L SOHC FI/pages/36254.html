Marker Lamp: Description and Operation

When you switch the headlight switch to PARK or HEAD, the taillight relay is energized. The relay contacts close, supplying battery voltage through fuse 49 and the RED/BLK wire to the side marker flasher circuit in the integrated control unit. The front side marker lights then come on. When the turn signals or hazard flasher are turned on, the left and right turn signal inputs to the control unit cause the side markers to flash. With the headlight switch OFF, the front side markers will flash with the turn signals; with the headlight switch in PARK or HEAD, they will flash in opposition to the turn signals.
