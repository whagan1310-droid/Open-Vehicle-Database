DTC 17

DTC 17 - VEHICLE SPEED SENSOR (VSS)




PART 1 OF 2




PART 2 OF 2

The Malfunction Indicator Lamp (MIL) indicates Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) 17: A problem in the Vehicle Speed Sensor (VSS) circuit.

The signal generated by the VSS, produces pulses when the front wheels turn.
