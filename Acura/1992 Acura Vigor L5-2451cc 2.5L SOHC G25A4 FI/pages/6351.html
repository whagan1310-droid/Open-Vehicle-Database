Rattles Behind the Driver Knee Side Bolster

Symptom:
Rattles behind the driver side bolster when driving over bumps.

Probable Cause:
Contact between a stud on the knee bolster and the dashboard brace.

Corrective Action:





1. Remove the lower dash cover and the knee bolster.






2. Put rubber tubing over the stud on the knee bolster.



3. Reinstall the knee bolster and lower dash cover.
