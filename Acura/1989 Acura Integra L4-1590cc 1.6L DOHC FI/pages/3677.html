Condenser Fan: Testing and Inspection

Diode

NOTE: The diodes are designed to pass current in one direction and block current in opposite direction.





1. Using an ohmmeter or continuity tester, check the diodes.
2. Check for continuity in both directions between 1 and 2 terminals.
There should be continuity in only one direction.
