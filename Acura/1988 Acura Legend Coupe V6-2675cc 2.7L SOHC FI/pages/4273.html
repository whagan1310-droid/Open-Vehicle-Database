Coupe

Self-diagnosis indicator light D comes on: Indicates a problem in the Function Control Motor Circuit.

The function control motor controls the outlet air direction and volume according to output from the control unit.

NOTE: Use the digital multimeter (KS-AHM-32-003) to check.
