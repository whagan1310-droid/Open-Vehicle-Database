Evaporative Emission Control Canister: Description and Operation

Evaporative Control Canister:





PURPOSE
Stores fuel vapors evaporating off the fuel in the fuel tank during periods of non-operation, and allows those vapors to be burned off during driving.

OPERATION
Vapors from the tank pass through tubing into the canister, where they are stored in activated charcoal.

CONSTRUCTION
A black plastic canister with a purge control diaphragm on it to seal in the vapors until the engine is running.
