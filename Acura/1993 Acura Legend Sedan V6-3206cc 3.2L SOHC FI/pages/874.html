Frequent Stalling During Warm-Up

CHECK:

1. Idle Air Control Valve

2. Idle adjusting screw

3. Fuel pressure regulator

4. Engine Coolant Temperature Sensor

5. Throttle cable adjustment


NOTE: Sub-systems are ordered according to greatest likelihood of failure and should be checked in the order that they are listed.
