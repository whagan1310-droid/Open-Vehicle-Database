General System Description

Fuel System Components:





FUEL INJECTION
The Programmed Fuel Injection (PGM-FI) system is an electronically controlled multi-point sequential fuel injection system. Injection quantity is determined by the length of time the injector is open. Injection duration is controlled by the Engine Control Module (ECM). The ECM contains a basic injection program which is modified by various input signals to achieve a precise injection duration. Refer to "COMPUTERS AND CONTROL SYSTEMS" for additional information regarding electronic controls.

Intake Air System:





AIR INTAKE SYSTEM
The intake air system controls engine idling, fast idle, low speed, and high speed operating demands. Engine idle is ECM controlled by the Electronic Air Control Valve which is covered in "COMPUTERS AND CONTROL SYSTEMS." The idle system uses an AIR BOOST VALVE to assist engine starting and a FAST IDLE valve to increase idle speed during warm-up. Low speed and high speed power is optimized by an INTAKE CHAMBER VOLUME CONTROL SYSTEM.


NOTE: ECM CONTROLLED COMPONENTS.
These components are related to fuel delivery and engine air intake, however since they are "ECM" controlled they will be addressed in depth under "COMPUTERS AND CONTROL SYSTEMS".


ECM CONTROLLED COMPONENTS
1. Idle Air Control Valve (IAC)
2. Fast Idle Control Solenoid Valve
3. Fuel Pressure Regulator Cut-off Solenoid
4. Injector Resistor

For information regarding electrical or electronic controls, refer to COMPUTERS AND CONTROL SYSTEMS.
