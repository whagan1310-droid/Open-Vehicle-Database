Cigarette Lighter: Description and Operation

Battery voltage is applied at all times from fuse 9 to the normally open contacts of the cigarette lighter relay. With the ignition in ACC or RUN, battery voltage is applied through fuse 4 to the coil of the cigarette lighter relay. The coil is grounded at G302. The relay energizes and battery voltage is applied to the cigarette lighter. When you push the lighter in, its element completes the circuit to ground G501 and begins to heat up. When the element becomes sufficiently heated, it is spring-released and the circuit opens.
