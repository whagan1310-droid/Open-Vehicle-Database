Clutch Pressure Measurement In [S], [D] and [R]








Clutch Pressure Measurement in [S]. [D] and [R]
- Set the parking brake and block both rear wheels securely.
- Raise the front of the car and support with safety stands.
- Allow the front wheels to rotate freely.
- Run the engine at 2,000 rpm.
- Connect a vacuum pump to the manifold vacuum tube of the vacuum modulator valve, and apply a vacuum of 500-600 mmHg.
- Measure each clutch line pressure (under condition equivalent to throttle valve full close).
- Disconnect the vacuum tube from the vacuum modulator valve.
- Measure each clutch line pressure (under condition equivalent to throttle valve full open).

NOTE: Before testing, be sure the throttle B pressure is held within the specified limits.
