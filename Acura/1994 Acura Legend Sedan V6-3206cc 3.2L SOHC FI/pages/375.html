Main Catalytic Converter

Catalytic Converter Cutaway:




PURPOSE
Removes CO, HC, and NOx from the exhaust stream and converts them to CO2, N2 and H20.

Converter Efficiency VS. Stoichiometric Fuel/Air Ratio:





OPERATION
The catalytic converter removes CO, HC, and NOx from the exhaust stream most effectively at the stoichiometric (14.7 to 1 plus or minus 1%) fuel-air mixture ratio and at temperatures between 400 to 800° C (750 to 1500° F).

CONSTRUCTION
A monolythic three-way device that uses catalytic compounds applied to an integrally constructed honeycomb carrier surface in the center of the exhaust pipe.
