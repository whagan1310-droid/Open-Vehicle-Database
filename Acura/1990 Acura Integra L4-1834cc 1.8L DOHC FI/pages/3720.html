Fuel Pressure Control Solenoid: Description and Operation

The pressure regulator cut-off solenoid is mounted the the firewall in the rear of the engine compartment. The solenoid is power by ignition switch terminal 1 whenever the ignition is turned on. If coolant temperature is above 194°F (90°C) and intake air is above 176°F (80°C), the ECU provides a ground on terminal A10 which energizes the solenoid. When powered, vacuum to the fuel pressure regulator is bled off to the atmosphere, which raises the fuel pressure for approximately 60 seconds. This increase in fuel pressure is used to compensate for hot soak conditions that occur at high engine temperatures.
