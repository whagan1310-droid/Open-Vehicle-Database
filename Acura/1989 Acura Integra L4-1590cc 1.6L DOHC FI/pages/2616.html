Equipment Hookup & Adjustment Procedures


1590cc: With engine idling, disconnect the AIS connector. Adjust speed to specified setting value. Reconnect AIS and remove "Hazard" fuse at battery + post for 10 seconds minimum. Check that idle speed is at specified checking value. Fast idle is not adjustable. 1988, turn on headlights and rear window defroster. Verify that Electrical load speed-up is at specified value. Turn on AC, blower on high, verify that AC speed-up is at specified value.
