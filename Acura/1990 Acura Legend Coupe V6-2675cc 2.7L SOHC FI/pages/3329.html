Cabin Temperature Sensor / Switch: Service and Repair

IN-CAR TEMPERATURE SENSOR
The in-car temperature sensor assembly includes a small fan (aspirator fan) to draw air past the sensor.

Removal:
1. Remove the console. Service and Repair





2. Remove the mounting screws (3) and remove the in-car sensor assembly.

NOTE: Be careful not to damage the center console.

Installation:
Install the in-car temperature sensor assembly in the reverse order of removal.
