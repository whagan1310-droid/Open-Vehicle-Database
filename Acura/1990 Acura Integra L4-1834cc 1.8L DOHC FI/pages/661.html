Ignition System: Application and ID

ENGINE DESIGNATION [1] B18A1

EMISSION CERTIFICATIONS [2]
FEDERAL Starting from engine #1300001

FUEL SYSTEM [3] PGM SFI

IGNITION SYSTEM [4] PGM IG

[1] The engine designation (first five digits of engine number) is located on the top RH side of engine block.
[2] The emission certification (the last 7 digits of the engine number) is located on the top RH side of engine block.
[3] Programmed sequential multi-point fuel injection.
[4] Programmed ignition.
[5] Programmed electronic fuel injection.
