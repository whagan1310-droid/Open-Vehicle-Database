Changing Transmission Fluid

1. Drive vehicle until engine reaches operating temperature.
2. Park vehicle on level ground and turn engine off.
3. Remove transaxle drain plug and drain fluid into suitable container.
4. Install drain plug with new washer, then refill transmission to FULL mark on dipstick.
