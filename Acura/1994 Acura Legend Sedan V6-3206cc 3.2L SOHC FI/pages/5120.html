DTC 1-2

NOTE:
- If the indicator light indicates DTC 1-2, replace the TCS control unit.
- If the TCS indicator light stays on, check for a short to body ground in the BLU/RED wire between the TCS control unit and the TCS indicator light. If the wire is OK, replace the TCS control unit.
