120000 MI or 192000 KM

Check
    Valve Clearance
      Adjust as necessary.
    Idle Speed
    Brakes and Traction Control
      Inspect front and rear calipers, pads, discs, hoses, and lines.
    Antilock Brakes / Traction Control Systems
Inspect
    Suspension
      Inspect suspension mounting bolts. Tighten as necessary.
    Steering
      Inspect steering operation, tie rod ends, steering gearbox and boots.
    Power Steering
    Positive Crankcase Ventilation Valve
      If clicking is heard as you pinch the hose between PCV valve and intake manifold, the valve is OK.
    Parking Brake System
      Adjust as necessary. Should be fully engaged in 10 to 14 clicks.
    Fuel Supply Line
      Inspect fuel pipes, hoses and connections.
    Exhaust System
    Drive Belt
      Inspect alternator drive belt. Adjust as necessary.
    Cooling System
      Inspect hoses and connections.
    Catalyst Shield
    Brake Pad
      Inspect front brake pads.
    Alignment
Replace
    Spark Plug
    Oil Filter, Engine
    Fuel Filter
    Fluid - M/T
    Fluid - A/T
    Engine Oil
    Brake Hose/Line
      Replace ABS Pressure Hose (ABS cars only).
    Brake Fluid
      Replace brake fluid, including anti-lock brake fluid.
    Air Filter Element
