Radio "Code" Theft Protection

NOTE: The original radio has a coded theft protection circuit. Be sure to get the customer's code number before
a. Disconnecting the battery.
b. Removing the fuse from the under-hood fuse/relay box.
c. Removing the radio.
d. After service, reconnect power to the radio and turn it on. When the word "CODE" is displayed, enter the customer's 5-digit code to restore radio operation.
