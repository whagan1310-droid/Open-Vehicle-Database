Torque Converter Clutch Solenoid: Description and Operation

The lock-up control solenoid is located inside the transmission housing. The solenoid is used to correctly time torque converter lock-up. This typically occurs at cruising speeds in high gear with low engine load. The ECU applies power at pin A8 which energizes the lock-up solenoid. When energized, transmission fluid is allowed to the lock-up piston which enable a direct connection between engine and drivetrain by eliminating any torque converter slippage.
