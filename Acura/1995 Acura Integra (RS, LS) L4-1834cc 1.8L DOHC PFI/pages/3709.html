Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) 6-1: Front Fail-safe Relay Diagnosis
The ABS control unit monitors the voltage from the battery for the six solenoids during the initial diagnosis when the fail-safe relays are OFF.

The ABS control unit keeps the ABS indicator light on if it detects battery voltage at the front solenoid circuits.

Possible causes:

- Front fail-safe relay stuck ON

- Short to power in the solenoid drive circuits between the front fail-safe relay and ABS control unit
