DTC 2




NOTE:
- Before proceeding to the troubleshooting procedure, be sure to perform a running test and check whether the TCS indicator light comes on or not. If the TCS indicator light does not come on, the system is working properly. If the TCS indicator light comes on, make sure the parking brake lever is released.
- When both of the rear wheels are running at 6 mph (10 km/h) or over and the parking brake is applied for more than 30 seconds continuously, the TCS control unit will cause the TCS indicator light to come on.
