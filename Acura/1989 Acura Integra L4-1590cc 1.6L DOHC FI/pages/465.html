Fuel Filter: Testing and Inspection

Check Fuel Filter for leaks or damage to filter body. Remove Fuel Filter and check for restriction or blockage, refer to "COMPONENT REPLACEMENT AND REPAIR PROCEDURES" for removal procedure. Because the Fuel Filter is a normal service item, it is recommended that the Fuel Filter be replaced at it's normal service interval or if a defect is suspected. Refer to "MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES".
