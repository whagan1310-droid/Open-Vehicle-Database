Electronic Brake Control Module: Service and Repair

Fig. 101 Control Unit Replacement:





1. Remove rear seat back.
2. Remove control unit attaching bolts and unit, Fig. 101, noting the following:
a. Control unit memory is cleared when bolts are removed.
b. Handle control unit with caution.
3. Reverse procedure to install. Torque control unit attaching bolts to 7 ft. lbs. Check anti-lock warning indicator by turning ignition switch to On position.
