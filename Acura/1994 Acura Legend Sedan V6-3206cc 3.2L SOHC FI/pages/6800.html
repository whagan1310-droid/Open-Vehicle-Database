Brake Signal: Adjustments

Brake Light Switch Location And Adjustment:





Disconnect the brake switch electrical connector. Screw in the brake switch until its plunger is fully depressed (threaded end touching the pad on the pedal arm). Then back off the switch 1/4 turn to produce 0.3 mm (0.01 inches) of clearance between the threaded end and pad. Tighten the locknut firmly. Connect the brake switch connector.
