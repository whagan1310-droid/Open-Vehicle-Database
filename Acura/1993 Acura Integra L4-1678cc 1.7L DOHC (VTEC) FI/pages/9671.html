Distributor Cap: Service and Repair

DISTRIBUTOR CAP REMOVAL AND INSTALLATION.

1. With ignition switch off, remove the 3 distributor cap screws.
2. Remove the spark plug wires from the old cap and place on the new cap one plug wire at a time to prevent cross firing.
3. Install the 3 distributor cap screws.
