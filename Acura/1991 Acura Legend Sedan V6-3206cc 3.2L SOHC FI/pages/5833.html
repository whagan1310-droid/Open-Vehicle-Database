Intake Air Duct: Description and Operation

Air Cleaner:





The air intake duct provides a path for fresh air to enter the air cleaner assembly. It is located beneath and to the right of the air cleaner assembly.
The lower portion of the air intake duct contains one of two resonators employed to reduce intake air noise.
