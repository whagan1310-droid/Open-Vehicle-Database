Compressor HVAC

LUBRICANT CAPACITY
cc 80
U.S. oz. 2.7

REFRIGERANT OIL TO DRAIN DURING COMPRESSOR REPLACEMENT
When a new compressor is installed, drain 30 cc (1 fl oz) of refrigerant oil from the suction fitting on the compressor, unless you are replacing additional A/C components. Then pro-rate the amount you drain by the amount you should add for the other part(s).
