DTC 7








Self-diagnosis Red LED indicator blinks seven times.
Most likely a problem in the Throttle Angle Sensor Circuit.

The throttle angle sensor is a potentiometer. It is connected to the primary throttle valve shaft. As the throttle angle changes, the throttle angle sensor varies the voltage signal to the ECU.
