Ignition Coil



IGNITION COIL RESISTANCE:
Primary [1] 0.9-1.1 ohms

IGNITION TIMING:
M/T [2] 15~ +/- 2~
A/T [3] 15~ +/- 2~

[1] Between the A and B terminals.
[2] M/T: BTDC (RED) at 800 +/- 50 rpm in neutral.
[3] A/T: BTDC (RED) at 750+/-50 rpm in gear.
