Vehicle Speed Sensor: Service and Repair



Replacement





1. Remove the 6 mm bolt from the transmission housing and remove the mainshaft and countershaft speed sensors.
2. Replace the 0-rings with new ones before reassembling the mainshaft and countershaft speed sensors.

NOTE: Install the washer to the countershaft speed sensor before reassembling the couuntershaft speed sensor.
