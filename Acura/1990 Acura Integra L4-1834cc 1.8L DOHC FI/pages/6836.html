Sunvisor - Damaged By Motorized Seat Belt


91acura03

YEAR MODEL VIN APPLICATION
1990 INTEGRA See VEHICLES
AFFECTED


April 29, 1991

BULLETIN NO. 91-025




Sunvisor Damaged By Seat Belt

PROBLEM

The sunvisor can be pivoted under the track for the motorized seat belt. It may then be damaged when the belt moves forward.


VEHICLES AFFECTED

3-dr. up to JH4DA9 ... LS060333
4-dr. up to JH4DB1 ... LS036931


CORRECTIVE ACTION

Change the angle of the sunvisor arm by installing the tapered shim listed under PARTS INFORMATION.

1. Remove the sunvisor.

2. Pull the headliner down slightly in the area of the sunvisor mount with a right-angle scribe or offset screwdriver.

3. Insert the tapered shim between the headliner and the roof panel with the thick part of the shim facing forward.

4. Reinstall the sunvisor. Replace if damaged.

5. Repeat steps 1 - 4 on the other sunvisor.


PARTS INFORMATION

Sunvisor shim (2 required):
P/N 83230-SK7-308


WARRANTY CLAIM INFORMATION

Out-of-warranty: Any repair performed after warranty expiration may be eligible for goodwill consideration by the District Service Manager. You must request consideration, and get the DSM's decision, before starting work.
