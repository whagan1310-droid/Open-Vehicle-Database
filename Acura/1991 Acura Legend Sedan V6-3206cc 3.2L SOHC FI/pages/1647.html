Component Locations

Keyless Entry Component Locations

Door Switch: Part of door lock latch assembly, in respective door
Ignition Key Switch: Part of ignition switch, on steering column
In-Line Fuse: Behind dash, left of steering column
Keyless Entry Control Unit: On left lower dash panel
Left Front Door Lock Latch Assembly: In rear of left front door
Left Front Door Lock Switch: Part of door lock latch assembly
Left Rear Door Lock Latch Assembly: In rear of left rear door
Power Door Lock Control Unit: Behind dash, right of steering column
Right Front Door Lock Latch Assembly: In right front door
Right Front Door Lock Switch: Part of door lock latch assembly
Right Rear Door Lock Latch Assembly: In rear of right rear door
Security Control Unit: Behind dash, right of steering column
Under-Dash Fuse/Relay Box: Behind left kick panel
Under-Hood Fuse/Relay Box: Left side of engine compartment
