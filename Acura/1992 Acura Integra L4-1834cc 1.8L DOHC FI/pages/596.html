Pressure, Vacuum and Temperature Specifications

FAST IDLE VALVE
Operating temp. (coolant) Below 86 degrees F (30 degrees C)

FUEL PRESSURE REGULATOR [1]
Pressure at idle 43 - 50 psi (300 - 350 kPa)

FUEL PUMP
System operating pressure at idle 38 psi (260 kPa)
Displacement 230 cc minimum in 10 seconds
Relief valve opening pressure 64 - 85 psi (450 - 600 kPa)

[1] Vacuum hose disconnected.
