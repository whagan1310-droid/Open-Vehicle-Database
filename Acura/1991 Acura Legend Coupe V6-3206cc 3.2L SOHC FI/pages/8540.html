Cruise Control Servo: Service and Repair

1. Pull back boot and loosen the locknut, then disconnect cable from bracket.
2. Disconnect cable end from actuator rod, then 4-P connector from actuator.
3. Pull vent hose from grommet and disconnect vacuum hose from check valve.
4. Remove two mounting bolts and actuator with bracket and reservoir.
5. Reverse procedure to install.
