Aspirator: Service and Repair

NOTE: SRS wire harness is routed near the console.

WARNING: All SRS wire harnesses and connectors are colored yellow. Do not use electrical test equipment on these circuit.

CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the SRS wire harnesses when servicing the console.

1. Remove the climate control unit.





2. Disconnect the aspirator fan connector as shown.
3. Remove its mounting screws, then remove the aspirator fan.
