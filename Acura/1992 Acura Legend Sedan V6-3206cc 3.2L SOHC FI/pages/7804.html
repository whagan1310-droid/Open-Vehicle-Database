Cruise Control Switch: Service and Repair

1. Remove four screws, then remove instrument panel from dashboard.
2. Remove screw from rear of instrument panel, then the switch.
3. Turn socket 45° counterclockwise to remove socket.
4. Reverse procedure to install.
