Compressor Controls and Fans

The A/C ON input at cavity (A3) of the fan control unit is grounded through the A/C triple pressure switch and the climate control unit at G303. The fan control unit supplies two signals to the powertrain control module (PCM), indicating that the A/C system has been turned on and that the engine idle speed must be increased.

The fan control unit and the powertrain control module (PCM) are grounded from the (PDSW) terminals through the middle pressure switch (in the A/C triple pressure switch) to G153.

With the ignition switch in RUN, the powertrain control module (PCM) energizes the A/C compressor clutch relay by providing ground for it. The compressor clutch is then energized through fuse 3 and the closed contacts of the clutch relay.

The fan control unit uses the radiator fan control sensor to measure the engine's coolant temperature:

^ When the radiator's coolant temperature is above 84°C (183°F), both the condenser and radiator fans will run at low speed.

^ When the radiator's coolant temperature is above 90°C (194°F), the fan control unit causes both fans to run at high speed.

^ When the radiator's temperature exceeds 1090 C (268°F),the fan control unit, through the powertrain control module (PCM), deenergizes the clutch relay and the compressor clutch.

When the refrigerant pressure becomes too high (due to blockage), or too low (due to leakage), the A/C triple pressure switch will open. This prevents the clutch relay and the compressor clutch from energizing.

The radiator fan control sensor will disengage the compressor clutch if the coolant temperature exceeds 110°C (230°F).
