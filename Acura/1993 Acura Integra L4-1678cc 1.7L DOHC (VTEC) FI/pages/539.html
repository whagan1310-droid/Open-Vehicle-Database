Intake Air System

PURPOSE
The system supplies air for all engine needs.

CONSTRUCTION
It consists of the intake air pipe, air cleaner, intake air duct, throttle body, Idle Air Control (IAC) valve, fast idle thermo valve, and intake manifold. A resonator in the intake air pipe provides additional silencing as air is drawn into the system.
