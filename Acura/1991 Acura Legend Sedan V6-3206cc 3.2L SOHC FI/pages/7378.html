Tools - Damper Spring Compressor

August 19, 1991

BULLETIN NO.
91-042

YEAR
1991

MODEL
LEGEND

VIN APPLICATION
ALL

Damper Spring Compressor

A new tool is needed to properly service the front dampers on the 1991 Legend Sedan and Coupe. Use the Branick(R) MST-580A Strut Spring Compressor when replacing the damper unit, spring, or damper components. This tool is necessary due to the increased spring length, coil diameter, and spring preload.
