Reading Diagnostic Trouble Codes





Self-Diagnosis Indicator
When the Check Engine warning light has been reported on, turn the ignition ON, pull down the inspection window from under the passenger seat and observe the Red LED on the top of the ECU. The Red LED indicates a system failure code by blinking frequency.
(The Yellow LED is for idle speed adjustment and is not related to the following troubleshooting codes.)
