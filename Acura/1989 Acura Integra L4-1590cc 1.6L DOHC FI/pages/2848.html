Automatic transmission 700 +- 50 RPM [2]

Mounting nut torque 16 lb/ft (22 Nm, 2.2 kg/m)

Deflection 0.39 - 0.47 in (10 - 12 mm) [3]

[1] Engine at idle, vacuum hoses disconnected.
