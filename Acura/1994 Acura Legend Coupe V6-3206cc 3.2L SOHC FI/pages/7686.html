Fuel Pressure Control Solenoid: Description and Operation

PURPOSE
Controls the flow of vacuum to the fuel pressure regulator during certain conditions, causing the pressure to rise. This minimizes the possibility of vapor lock, and enriches the mixture.

OPERATION
Upon command from the Engine Control Module (ECM), the valve is energized, and vacuum is removed from the fuel pressure regulator. This occurs during hot starts and/or with high intake manifold temperature. The vacuum present in the hose leading to the regulator is vented through the solenoid.

CONSTRUCTION
An electrically operated valve that contains inlet and outlet ports, as well as a venting port.
