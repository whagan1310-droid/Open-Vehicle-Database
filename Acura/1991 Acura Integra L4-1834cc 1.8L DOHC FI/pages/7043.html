Rear Window Motor Test

Fig. 11 Rear Wiper Motor Connector:





1. Remove hatch trim panel.
2. Disconnect rear wiper motor electrical connector, Fig. 11.
3. Connect positive battery voltage to terminal No. 2 and negative to terminal No. 4, motor operation should be indicated.
4. If operation is not as indicated, replace motor.
