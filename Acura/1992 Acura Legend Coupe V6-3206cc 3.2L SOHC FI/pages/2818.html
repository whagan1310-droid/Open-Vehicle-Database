Electronic Noise Suppressor


IGNITION COIL RESISTANCE:
Primary [1] 0.9-1.1 ohms

IGNITION TIMING:
M/T [2] 15~ +/- 2~
A/T [3] 15~ +/- 2~

NOISE CONDENSER CAPACITY:
Capacity 0.47 +/- 0.09 microfarads.
