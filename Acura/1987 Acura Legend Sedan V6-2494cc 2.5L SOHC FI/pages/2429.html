Alternator - Low Charge Rate


89Acura04

YEAR MODEL VIN APPLICATION
1986-88 LEGEND See Affected
Sedan & Coupe Vehicles

August 7, 1989

BULLETIN NO.

89-018

Legend Low Charge Rate

PROBLEM

Repeated battery discharge due to short slow speed trips.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Remove and replace the alternator pulley and belt (see section 23 of the Legend service manual) with a new pulley and belt listed under PARTS INFORMATION.

AFFECTED VEHICLES

(49 ST)
