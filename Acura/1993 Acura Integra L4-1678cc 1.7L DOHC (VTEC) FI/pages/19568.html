Fog/Driving Lamp: Description and Operation

With the headlight switch in HEAD and the dimmer switch in LOW, voltage is provided to the fog light switch. When the fog light switch is turned on, the fog light relay is energized, providing voltage to the fog lights and the fog lights go on. The fog light indicator in the switch also goes on. If the dimmer switch is moved to HIGH, the fog lights will go off.
