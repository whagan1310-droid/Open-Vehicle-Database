Ignition System: Application and ID

DISPLACEMENT 3.0 Liter (2977cc)

CYLINDERS V6

ENGINE DESIGNATION [1] C30A1

FUEL SYSTEM [2] PGM SFI

IGNITION SYSTEM [3] DIS

COMPUTER SYSTEM [4] PGM FI

[1] The engine designation (first five digits of engine number) is located on the top RH side of engine block.
[2] Programmed sequential multi-point fuel injection.
[3] Direct Ignition System.
[4] Programmed electronic fuel injection.
