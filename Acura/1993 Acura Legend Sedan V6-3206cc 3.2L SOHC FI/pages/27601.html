R-12

NOTE:
The NSX R-134a piping has inch threads, therefore requiring an identification label. The Legend piping has metric threads with no label. Do not substitute R-12 parts for R-134a parts.

Reason for Change
R-134a refrigerant requires the H-NBR type O-rings to to seal the pipe fittings.
