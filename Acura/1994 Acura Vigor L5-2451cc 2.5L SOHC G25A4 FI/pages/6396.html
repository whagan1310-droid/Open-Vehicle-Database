Charge Lamp/Indicator: Testing and Inspection

1. Ensure alternator belt is properly adjusted and that all electrical connections are secure and free from corrosion. Check underdash fuse No. 2.
2. Observe warning lamp with ignition on and engine not running.
3. If warning lamp does not light, disconnect electrical connector from alternator and connect white/blue wire in terminal to ground.
4. If lamp still does not light, check the following:
a. Defective bulb.
b. Open in black/yellow wire between warning lamp and fuse panel or between fuse panel and ignition switch.
c. Open in white/blue wire between warning lamp and voltage regulator.
5. If lamp lights in step 3, refer to Charging System / Testing and Inspection / Procedures.
6. Run engine at idle and observe warning lamp.
7. If lamp remains lit, check condition of yellow/blue wire between relay box and alternator. If fuse and wire are satisfactory, refer to Charging System / Testing and Inspection / Procedures.
