Clutch: Adjustments

Fig. 2 Clutch pedal adjustments. Legend:





1. Measure clutch pedal disengagement height, pedal height, freeplay and stroke at pedal, Fig. 2.
2. Loosen locknut (A), then back off pedal switch until it no longer contacts clutch pedal.
3. Loosen locknut (B), then turn push rod in or out as necessary to provide specified stroke and pedal height measurements.
4. Tighten locknut (B), then screw in pedal switch until it contacts clutch pedal.
5. Turn switch in an additional 1/4-1/2 turn, then tighten locknut (A).
