Radio/Stereo: Description and Operation

The stereo sound system includes:

Stereo radio/cassette player

Power antenna

Rear window antenna

Foot well bass speaker/power amplifier

Driver's and passenger's door speaker/power amplifiers

Compact disc changer

Battery voltage is supplied at all times through fuse 33 to the stereo radio/cassette player for memory and power circuits. With the ignition in ACC or RUN, battery voltage is supplied through fuse 8 to the stereo radio/cassette player for audio and tape operation. With the stereo radio/cassette player switched on, the power antenna and power amplifier relay coil receive battery voltage through the stereo radio/cassette player. Battery voltage is then applied to each speaker/power amplifier through fuse 51 and the power amplifier relay contacts. The foot well bass speaker/power amplifier receives both left and right channel information from the stereo radio/cassette player. The door speaker/power amplifiers receive either left or right channel audio signals. The power amplifiers are grounded through G402. The compact disc changer receives power, ground and control inputs from the stereo radio/cassette player. The disc changer then sends its audio signal back through the stereo radio/cassette player to the speaker/amplifiers.
