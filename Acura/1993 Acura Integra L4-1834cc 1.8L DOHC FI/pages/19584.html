Interior Lighting Module: Description and Operation

The integrated control unit consists of the following circuits combined in a single control unit.

Refer to the section listed in parentheses for the remainder of the circuit:


^ Windshield Wiper/Washer (Intermittent Wiper Control Circuit)

^ Turn Signal and Hazard Lights (Side Marker Flasher Relay Circuit)


^ Seat Belt (Canada), Key-on Reminders (Seat Belt/Key-on Beeper Circuit)

Note: USA model seat belt information is found in the Automatic Shoulder Seat Belt section.


^ Lights-on Reminders (Lights-on Reminder Circuit)

^ Low Oil Pressure Indicator System (Low Oil Pressure Flasher Circuit)

^ Rear Window Defogger (Rear Window Defogger Timer Circuit)
