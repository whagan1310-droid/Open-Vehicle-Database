Ignition Coil: Specifications

IGNITION COIL RESISTANCE:
Primary [1] 0.3-0.4 ohms
Secondary [2] 9,040-13,560 ohms
Secondary [3] 2,090-2,310 ohms

IGNITION TIMING:
Manual Transmission [4] 15~+/-2~
Automatic Transmission [4] 15~+/-2~

SPARK PLUG WIRES [5] 25K ohms max.

[1] Between the A and D terminals.
[2] Between the A and secondary winding terminals.
[3] Between the B and D terminals.
[4] BTDC (RED) at 680+/-50 rpm in neutral.
[5] Test at 70~F (20~C).
