Horn: Description and Operation

! WARNING !

^ Do not use electrical test equipment on the yellow SRS wires and connectors in the steering column, console, and dashboard. You could unintentionally set-off the airbag, which could cause injury to you and others.

^ While troubleshooting or repairing other systems, be careful not to damage SRS wiring or components. Such damage could make the airbag inoperative, which could lead to the driver's injury or death if the car were in a severe frontal collision.

Voltage is applied at all times through fuse 45 to the horn relay. When you press a horn button, the horn switch closes, providing a ground for the coil in the horn relay. This energizes the horn relay, which applies battery voltage to all three horns and the horns sound.
