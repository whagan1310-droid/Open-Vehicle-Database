M/T - Breather Tube Position

October 6, 1992

YEAR
1991-92

MODEL
NSX

VIN APPLICATION
ALL with MANUAL TRANS

BULLETIN NO
92-022

Transmission Breather Tube Position (Supersedes 92-022, dated September 8, 1992)

PROBLEM

The open end of the transmission breather tube sits too close to the transmission case. This may allow moisture on the case to be drawn into the transmission as it cools.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Inspect and re-position the end of the transmission breather tube. Inspect all customer cars that come in for maintenance and all cars in dealer stock.

1. Measure how far the breather tube extends beyond its bracket, If it is 20 mm or less, go to step 3.

2. If the measurement is greater than 20 mm, pull the tube up through the bracket until the length is 20 mm. Do not bend the bracket, you may break the spot weld.

3. Paint a white spot on the breather tube at the top of the loop.

NOTE:
If the customer has complained of transmission noise while cornering, drain and refill the transmission.
