Ignition System: Specifications

IGNITION COIL RESISTANCE:
Primary [1] 0.6 - 0.8 ohms
Secondary [2] 12,800 - 19,200 ohms

IGNITION TIMING:
1.8L DOHC Engine [3] 16° ± 2°
1.7L DOHC VTEC Engine [4] 16° ± 2°

SPARK PLUG WIRES [5] 25K ohms max.

[1] Between the A and B terminals.
[2] Between the A and secondary winding terminals.
[3] BTDC (RED) at 750±50 rpm in neutral.
[4] BTDC (RED) at 800 ± 50 rpm in neutral.
[5] Test at 70°F (20°C).
