Condenser: Testing and Inspection

INSPECTION





1. Use a commercially available condenser tester. Connect the condenser tester probes and measure the condenser capacity.

Condenser capacity: 0.47 ± 0.09 microfarads

NOTE: The noise condenser is intended to reduce ignition noise. However, condenser failure may cause the engine to stop running.

2. If not within the specifications, replace the noise condenser.
