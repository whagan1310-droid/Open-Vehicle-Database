Heated Element: Description and Operation

The outside mirrors are heated to remove fog and frost.


Battery voltage is applied at all times to the contacts of the power mirror defogger relay through fuse 37. The defogger relay and switch are grounded at G401. When the ignition switch is in its RUN or START position, voltage is applied through fuse 8 to the defogger switch.


When you turn on the defogger switch, voltage is provided to the defogger indicator light, and the light comes on. The power mirror defogger relay coil is also energized and the relay's contacts close.


Voltage is provided through fuse 37 and the defogger relay to the heating elements in the mirrors.
