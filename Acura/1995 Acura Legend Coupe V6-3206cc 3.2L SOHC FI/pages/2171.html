Seats: Description and Operation

Power is provided at all times through fuse 52 to the front passenger's power seat switch.

Slide Operation
When you move the slide switch to FORWARD, power is supplied to the slide motor through the front passenger's power seat switch. The slide motor is grounded through the FORWARD contact of the slide switch. The motor will run until you release the switch or the seat reaches the full forward position. The contacts for BACKWARD operation of the slide switch are wired the opposite way of the FORWARD contacts, causing the motor to run in the opposite direction.

Recline Operation
Recline operation is similar to the slide operation.
