Fuel Pump Ballast Resistor: Testing and Inspection

Fuel Pump Resistor:





If the fuel pump will not run, install a jumper wire in place of the fuel pump resistor. If the fuel pump starts with the jumper installed, replace the fuel pump resistor. If the pump does not start, refer to MAIN RELAY.
