Clutch Master Cylinder: Service and Repair

1. Disable coded theft protection system as described under Accessories and Optional Equipment / Antitheft and Alarm Systems / Service and Repair.
2. Disarm airbag system as described under Air Bags and Seat Belts / Air Bags (Supplemental Restraint Systems) / Airbag System Disarming.
3. Working from underneath dash panel, pry out cotter pin, then remove clutch pedal pivot pin from yoke.
4. Remove attaching nuts at dash panel, disconnect hoses, then remove master cylinder from vehicle. Plug hoses to prevent fluid loss.
5. Reverse procedure to install. Adjust clutch as outlined under Adjustments.
6. Restore coded theft protection system and rearm airbag.
