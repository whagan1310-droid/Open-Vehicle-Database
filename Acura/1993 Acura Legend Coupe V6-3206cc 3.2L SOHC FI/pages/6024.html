Cellular Phone: Description and Operation

Battery voltage is supplied at all times through fuse 56 to the transceiver. With the ignition switch in ACC or RUN, battery voltage is supplied through fuse 4 to the transceiver and to the control box. The transceiver is grounded at G451.


Hands Free Operation (using steering wheel controls)


^ To receive a call, press the Mode switch on the steering wheel controls.


^ To mute a call, press the Aud/Tel on the steering wheel controls.


^ To switch to hands-free operation after initially lifting the handset, press the Aud/Tel switch and replace the handset to its mount.


^ To place a call, follow these steps:

1. Press the mode switch to enter the phone mode and mute the speaker The phone will ask for "location please."


2. Press the up and down switches to scroll through the first ten memory locations.


The phone will say 01, 02, 03, 04....10.


3. Upon reaching the desired location, press the Aud/Tel switch to verify the

contents of that location.


4. Upon verification, press the mode switch to initiate the call.
