Ignition Rotor: Service and Repair

ROTOR REPLACEMENT

1. With ignition switch off, remove distributor cap (3 screws).
2. Remove rotor retaining screw.
3. Remove rotor.
4. Install new rotor and retaining screw.
5. Re-attach distributor cap (3 screws).
