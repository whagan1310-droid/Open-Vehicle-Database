General Description

"Front End Alignment" refers to the angular relationship between the front wheels, the front suspension attaching parts and the ground. Proper front end alignment must be maintained in order to insure efficient steering, good directional stability and to prevent abnormal tire wear. The most important factors of front end alignment are wheel toe-in, wheel camber and axle caster.
