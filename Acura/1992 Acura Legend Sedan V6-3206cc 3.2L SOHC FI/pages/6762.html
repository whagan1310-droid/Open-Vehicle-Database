Seat Belt Pretensioner Installation




Seat Belt Pretensioner Installation

CAUTION:
- Be sure to install the harness wires so that they are not pinched or interfering with other parts.
- Be sure the battery cables are disconnected.

1. Install the seat belt pretensioner.






2. Reinstall the shoulder harness anchor bolt.






3. Remove the short connector (RED) from the pretensioner, then connect the side wire harness connector to the pretensioner.

4. Connect the 2-P pretensioner connector to the tension reducer connector.

5. Reinstall the B pillar lower panel, door opening trim, and door sill molding.






6. Remove the short connector (RED) from the front passenger's airbag and the short connector A from the SRS main harness.

7. Reconnect the front passenger 5 airbag connector to the SRS main harness connector, then reinstall the glove box.

8. Remove the short connector (RED) from the driver's airbag and the short connector A from the cable reel.

9. Reconnect the driver's airbag connector to the cable reel connector. Attach the short connector (RED) to the access panel, then reinstall the panel on the steering wheel.

10. Reconnect the battery positive cable, then the negative cable.

11. After installing the seat belt pretensioner, confirm proper system operation.

- Turn the ignition to II: the instrument panel SRS indicator light should go on for about 6 seconds and then go off.
- Confirm operation of the seat belt retractor.
- Confirm operation of the seat belt tension reducer.
Enter the customer's code number to restore radio operation.
