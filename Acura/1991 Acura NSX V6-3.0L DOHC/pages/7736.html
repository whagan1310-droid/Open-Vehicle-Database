Center Sun Visor - The Seam Splits

91acura01 January 28, 1991
YEAR MODEL
1991 NSX
VIN APPLICATION BULLETIN NO
All up to VIN
JH4NA1 ... MT000562 91-010
Split Sun Visor Seam

PROBLEM

The seam on the center sun visor splits.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Replace the center sun visor on all cars within the designated VIN range with the improved part listed in PARTS INFORMATION. Replacement should be done when the car is brought in for service.

PROCEDURE

1. Remove the screw at each end of the sun visor. then remove and discard the sun visor.

2. Install the improved part.

PARTS INFORMATION

Sunvisor: 83250-SL0-A01ZC Black 83250-SL0-A01ZD White
WARRANTY CLAIM INFORMATION

In warranty: The normal warranty applies.

Out-of-warranty: Any repair performed after warranty expiration may be eligible for goodwill consideration by the District Service Manager. You must request consideration, and get the DSM's decision, before starting work.
