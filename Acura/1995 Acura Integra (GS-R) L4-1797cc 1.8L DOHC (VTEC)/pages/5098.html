Air Temperature Sensor ( Ambient / Intake ): Service and Repair

WARNING: Perform this after the engine has cooled down to avoid burns.

1. Disconnect 2-wire connector (RED/YEL & GRN/BLU wires) from the sensor.
2. Using a screwdriver, remove the 2 mounting screws securing the sensor to the intake manifold.
3. Carefully remove the old sensor.
4. Install the new sensor.
5. Secure the sensor with the 2 mounting screws.
6. Re-connect the 2-wire connector.
