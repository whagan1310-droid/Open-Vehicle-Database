Crankshaft Position Sensor: Description and Operation

DESCRIPTION
The CKP Sensor determines timing for fuel injection and ignition of each cylinder and also detects engine speed. The CYP Sensor detects the position of No. 1 cylinder for sequential fuel injection to each cylinder and ignition timing.
