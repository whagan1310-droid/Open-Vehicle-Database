Electronic Brake Control Module: Service and Repair


1. Disconnect battery ground cable.
2. Remove rear seat back.
3. Disconnect electrical connectors, then remove attaching bolts and the control unit. When attaching bolts are removed, the control unit memory will be erased.
4. Reverse procedure to install. After installation is completed, turn ignition switch On and ensure that ANTI-LOCK warning light is illuminated.
