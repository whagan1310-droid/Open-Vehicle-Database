Audible Warning Device: Description and Operation

Seat Belt Reminder (Canadian models only)


With the ignition switch in RUN or START, voltage is applied to the seat belt reminder light. When you unbuckle the driver's seat belt, the integrated control unit senses ground at the YEL/RED wire of C407. Ground is also present at the YEL/RED wire of C714. When the belt is unbuckled, the reminder light goes on and the beeper sounds for 5 seconds.


Ignition Key-In Reminder


When the ignition key switch is closed (key is in the ignition), voltage is provided at the BLU/WHT wire of the integrated control unit. When you open the driver's door, ground is provided at the GRN/ BLU wire of the integrated control unit, which completes the circuit and sets off the beeper.


Lights-on Reminder


Voltage is applied at all times to the headlight switch. When the headlight switch is in PARK or HEAD, voltage is applied through fuse 11 to the integrated control unit. When you open the driver's door, the integrated control unit senses ground at the GRN/BLU wire of 0407. If voltage is present at fuse lithe lights-on chime module sounds.
