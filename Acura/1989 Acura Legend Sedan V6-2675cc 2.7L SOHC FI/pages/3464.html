Wiper Switch: Service and Repair

1. Disable coded theft protection system as described under Accessories and Optional Equipment / Antitheft and Alarm Systems / Service and Repair.
2. On models equipped with airbag, disarm airbag system as described under Air Bags and Seat Belts / Air Bags (Supplemental Restraint Systems) / Airbag System Disarming.
3. Remove steering wheel as described under Steering and Suspension / Steering / Steering Wheel / Service and Repair
4. Remove steering wheel lower cover.
5. On models equipped with cruise control, remove cruise control slip ring.
6. On all models, remove turn signal cancelling sleeve.
7. Remove upper column holder, bending plate, bending plate base and bending plate guide.
8. Disconnect combination switch electrical connector.
9. Remove combination switch attaching screws and the switch.
10. Reverse procedure to install.
11. Restore coded theft protection system and rearm airbag.
