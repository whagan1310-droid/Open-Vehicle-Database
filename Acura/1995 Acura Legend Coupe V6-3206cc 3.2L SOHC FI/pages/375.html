Cooling Fans Troubleshooting

ONLY ONE FAN OPERATES

1. Blown underhood fuse 47 or 20.
2. Faulty relay.
3. Faulty coolant temperature sensor.


FANS DO NOT ROTATE UNDER ALL CONDITIONS

1. Blown dash panel fuse 3.
2. Faulty coolant temperature sensor.
3. Faulty cooling fan control unit.
4. Poor or faulty ground connection.

FANS DO NOT OPERATE AT LOW SPEED (BLOWN DASH PANEL FUSE 15)

1. Blown dash panel fuse 20.
2. Faulty relay.
3. Faulty coolant temperature sensor.
4. Faulty cooling fan control unit.
5. Poor or faulty ground connection.

FANS DO NOT ROTATE AT HIGH SPEED

1. Blown dash panel fuse 3.
2. Faulty relay.
3. Faulty coolant temperature sensor.
4. Faulty cooling fan control unit.
5. Poor or faulty ground connection.

COMPRESSOR CLUTCH DOES NOT ENGAGE

1. Faulty A/C or ECU system.
2. Faulty A/C pressure switch.
3. Faulty oil temperature switch.
4. Faulty cooling fan control unit.
5. Poor or faulty ground connection.

FAN TIMER UNIT FUNCTIONS IMPROPERLY

1. Blown dash panel fuse 15.
2. Blown dash panel fuse 20.
3. Faulty A/C pressure switch.
4. Faulty oil temperature sensor.
5. Poor or faulty ground connection.
