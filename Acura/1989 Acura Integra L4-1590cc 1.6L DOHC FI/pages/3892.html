March 27, 1989
Buzzing Sound From Rear Speaker


PROBLEM Small pieces of the metal speaker cover fall onto the speakers and vibrate causing a buzzing sound.

AFFECTED VEHICLES

'88 Integra . . . . . . . ALL '89 Integra RS . . . . . ALL '89 Integra LS 3-dr . . . up to DA3-KS020880 '89 Integra LS 5-dr . . . up to DA1-KS004133

CORRECTIVE ACTION

1. Remove the rear speaker cover(s) as described in the appropriate service manual.

2. Remove the pieces of metal cover from the speaker(s) using a magnet. Be careful not to damage the speaker(s).

3. Turn the speaker cover(s) over and inspect the metal covers at their edges. Remove any pieces that may come loose and cause a similar problem in the future.
