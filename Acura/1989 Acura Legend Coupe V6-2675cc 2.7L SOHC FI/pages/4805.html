Coupe

ECU Removal Fig. 2:





1. Disconnect negative battery cable.
2. Lift carpet in front passenger footwell.
3. Remove cover screws.
4. Remove ECU mounting screws.
5. Remove ECU from vehicle.
6. Reverse procedure to install. Insure all connections to ECU are tight and secure.
