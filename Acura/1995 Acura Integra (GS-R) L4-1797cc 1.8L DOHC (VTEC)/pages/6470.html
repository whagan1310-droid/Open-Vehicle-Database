Clutch Slave Cylinder: Service and Repair

1. Disconnect pipe from slave cylinder. Plug opening in pipe to prevent fluid loss.
2. Remove slave cylinder to clutch case attaching bolts, then slave cylinder.
3. Reverse procedure to install, noting the following:
a. Lubricate end of pushrod with molybdenum disulfide grease.
b. Bleed clutch hydraulic system.
