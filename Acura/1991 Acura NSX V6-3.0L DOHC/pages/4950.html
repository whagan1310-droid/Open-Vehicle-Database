Fuel Injector Resistor: Testing and Inspection

Injector Resistor:





1. Disconnect the resistor connector.
2. Use an Ohm meter to check resistence between the power terminal (a) and each of the resistor terminals (b - g).

Resistence should be 5 - 7 Ohms.

3. If any resistence measurements are outside of specifications, replace the resistor.
