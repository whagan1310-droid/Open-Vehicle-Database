Instrument Cluster / Carrier: Service and Repair

On models equipped with airbag system, refer to Technician Safety Information for system disarming and arming procedures.

1. On models equipped with radio coded theft protection system, refer to Vehicle Damage Warnings for system disarming and arming procedures.
2. Remove instrument panel lower cover attaching screws, then pull rearward to unclip three upper clips.
3. Remove upper and lower steering column covers.
4. Carefully pry front fog lamp, speed control and rear defroster switches from instrument panel.
5. Remove the four instrument panel retaining screws, then detach the eight retaining clips.
6. Position suitable cloth over combination switch assembly.
7. Remove four instrument cluster attaching screws, then pull rearward.
8. Disconnect cluster electrical connectors.
9. Pull instrument cluster rearward and to lefthand side to remove.
10. Reverse procedure to install.
11. On models equipped with radio coded theft protection system, refer to Vehicle Damage Warnings for system disarming and arming procedures.
12. On models equipped with airbag system, refer to Technician Safety Information for system disarming and arming procedures.
