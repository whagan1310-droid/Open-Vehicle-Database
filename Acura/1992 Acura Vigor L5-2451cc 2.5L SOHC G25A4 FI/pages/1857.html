Instrument Cluster / Carrier: Service and Repair

Fig. 1 SRS Red Connector:





1. Disable coded theft protection system as described under Accessories and Optional Equipment / Antitheft and Alarm Systems / Service and Repair.
2. Disarm airbag system as described under Air Bags and Seat Belts / Air Bags (Supplemental Restraint Systems) / Airbag System Disarming.
3. Install SRS short red connector as shown in Fig. 1.
4. Remove instrument panel lower cover attaching screws, the pull rearward to unclip three upper clips.
5. Remove upper and lower steering column covers.
6. Carefully pry front fog lamp, speed control and rear defroster switches from instrument panel.
7. Remove four instrument cluster panel attaching screws.
8. Position suitable cloth over combination switch assembly.
9. Remove four instrument cluster attaching screws, then pull rearward.
10. Disconnect cluster electrical connectors.
11. Pull instrument cluster rearward and to lefthand side to remove.
12. Reverse procedure to install.
13. Restore coded theft protection system and rearm airbag.
