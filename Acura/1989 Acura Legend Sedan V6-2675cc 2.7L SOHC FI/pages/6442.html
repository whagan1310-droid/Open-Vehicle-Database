Electronic Noise Suppressor: Description and Operation


Radio Condenser Capacity Test:






The radio noise condenser is intended to reduce ignition noise in the radio. When electrical sparks are generated, electromagnetic waves are produced. These waves cause considerable interference to radio reception. The condenser also known as a "capacitor" acts as an electrical sponge, it absorbs electromagnetic waves. The radio noise condenser is located on the left fender, below the ignition coil.
