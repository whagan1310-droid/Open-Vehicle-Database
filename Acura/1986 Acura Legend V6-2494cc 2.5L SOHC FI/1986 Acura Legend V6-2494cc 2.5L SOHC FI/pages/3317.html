Floor MAT - Premature Wear

86acura01

November 3, 1986
YEAR MODEL APPLICATION
1986 LEGEND ALL
86-020
Premature Floor Mat Wear

SYMPTOM

Legend accessory floor mat sets manufactured prior to June 1, 1986 may exhibit premature wear.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Regardless of the condition of the floor mats, replace all four floor mats if the date of manufacture is prior to June 1, 1986.

How to Identify Date of Manufacture

Read the code stamped on the underside of the driver's floor mat to identify the month, day and year of manufacture. For example, a code stamped "030586" means the floor mats were manufactured on March 5, 1986.
