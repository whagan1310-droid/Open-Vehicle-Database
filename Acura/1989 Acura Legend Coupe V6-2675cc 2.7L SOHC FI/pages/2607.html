DTC 9

Code 9 indicates a malfunction in the CYL portion of the CRANK/CYL sensor or circuit.

1. Remove Alternator Sense fuse in underhood relay box for 10 seconds to reset ECU.
2. Start engine and observe diagnostic LED.

Flashes code 9, proceed to step 4.
Does not flash, proceed to next step.

3. Malfunction is intermittent. Check connections between ECU and CRANK/CYL sensor.


CYL Sensor Code 9 Diagnosis:






4. Disconnect CRANK/CYL sensor connector.
5. Measure resistance between ORN and WHT terminals as shown.

500 - 1000 ohms, proceed to next step.
Not 500 - 1000 ohms, replace CRANK/CYL sensor. End of test.

6. Check for continuity to ground between ORN and WHT wires individually.

Continuity, replace CRANK/CYL sensor. End of test.
No continuity, proceed to next step.

7. Reconnect CRANK/CYL sensor connector.
8. Install test harness to main harness only. If test harness is not available, carefully backprobe wires at ECU connector.


CYL Sensor Code 9 Diagnosis:






9. Check resistance between terminals C1 and C2.

500 - 1000 ohms, proceed to step 11.
Not 500 - 1000 ohms, proceed to next step.

10. Repair open in ORN or WHT wires between ECU and CRANK/CYL sensor. End of test.
11. Check terminal C1 for continuity to ground.

Continuity, proceed to next step.
No continuity, proceed to step 13.

12. Repair short to ground in ORN wire between ECU terminal C1 and CRANK/CYL sensor. End of test.
13. Replace electronic control unit.
