Component Locations

Fuel Delivery System:





FUEL CUT-OFF VALVE
Located on the top of the fuel tank.

FUEL FILTER
Located to the left-center of the firewall.

FUEL INJECTORS
Located above each intake valve and secured between the intake manifold and the fuel rail assembly.

FUEL LINES
Located under the left side of the vehicle from the fuel tank to the fuel manifold assembly.

FUEL PRESSURE REGULATOR
Attached to the left side of the rear fuel rail.

FUEL PUMP
Located in the fuel tank, accessed via the maintenance access cover.

FUEL RAIL
Two rails, one front and one rear, located above the fuel injectors.


FUEL TANK
Located under the left rear of the vehicle, ahead of the rear wheels.

INJECTOR RESISTOR
Located in the engine compartment at the right fender well area.
