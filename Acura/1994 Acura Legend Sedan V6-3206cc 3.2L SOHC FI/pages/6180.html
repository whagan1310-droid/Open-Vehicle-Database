Timing Belt: Testing and Inspection

Inspection
1. Remove the left upper cover.





2. Remove the damper from the center bracket.






3. Inspect the timing belt for cracks and oil or coolant soaking.

NOTE:
- Replace belt if oil soaked.
- Quickly remove any oil or solvent that gets on the belt.
