A/C Refrigerant Oil Replacement

Add refrigerant oil (ND OIL 6) after replacing the following parts:
- Condenser: 15 cc (2/3 fl oz)
- Evaporator: 30 cc (1/2 fl oz)
- Line or hose: 10 cc (1/3 fl oz)
- Receiver/dryer: 10 cc (1/3 fl oz)
- Compressor: 140 cc (4-2/3 fl oz) - Volume of oil from removed compressor = Volume to drain from new compressor.
On compressor replacement, subtract the volume of oil drained from the removed compressor from 140 cc (4-2/3 fl oz), and drain the calculated volume of oil from the new compressor.
