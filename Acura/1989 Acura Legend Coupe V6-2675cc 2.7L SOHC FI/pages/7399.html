Sunroof - Visor Vibration

88acura04

YEAR MODEL VIN APPLICATION
ALL LEGEND COUPE ALL with Accessory
Sunroof Visor
December 19, 1988

BULLETIN NO.

88-022 ACC
Sunroof Visor Vibration

SYMPTOM

A fluttering or buzzing heard while driving between 30 and 40 miles-per-hour with the sunroof open.


DIAGNOSIS

This sound can be eliminated by placing your hand on the back edge of the sunroof visor or closing the sunroof.


CORRECTIVE ACTION

Replace the sunroof visor with the new unit listed in PARTS INFORMATION.

NOTE: Due to vibration, the rubber seal on the original visor may have left marks in the paint. The replacement visor, since it is about 2 inches shorter, can leave them exposed. Before installing the new visor, clean and examine the mounting surface on the roof. If necessary, polish out any marks left by the old visor.


PARTS INFORMATION

Accessory Sunroof Visor for Legend Coupe: 08189-SG0A3AA

NOTE: This Part Number applies to Legend Coupe installations only. Sunroof Visor 08189-SG0A2AA should still be used for Legend Sedan and Integra applications.
