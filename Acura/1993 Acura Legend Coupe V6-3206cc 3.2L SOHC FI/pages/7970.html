Indicators

When the ignition is in RUN or START battery voltage is supplied through fuse 13 to the indicators in the gauge assembly. The dimming circuits and some of the indicators are grounded at G303. Most indicators are turned on by the circuits listed below them on the schematic. Refer to those circuits for operation and illumination details.
