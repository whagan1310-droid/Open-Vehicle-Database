Heated Element: Description and Operation

With the ignition switch in RUN, voltage is applied through fuse 19 to the mirror defogger switch. When the switch is turned on, voltage is provided to the ON indicator, causing it to come on. Voltage is also provided to the heating elements in the mirrors and the elements become hot to remove fog from the glass.
