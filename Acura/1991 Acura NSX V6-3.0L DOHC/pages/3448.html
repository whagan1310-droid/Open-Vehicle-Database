Thermostat: Testing and Inspection

Thermostat Testing:






NOTE: Replace thermostat if it is open at room temperature.

1. Suspend the thermostat in a container of water as shown.
2. Heat the water, and check the temperature with a thermometer. Check the temperature at which the thermostat first opens and at full lift.

CAUTION: Do not let thermometer touch the bottom of the hot container.

3. Measure lift height of the thermostat when fully open.
^ Lift height: above 10 mm (0.39 inch)
^ Starts opening: 169 - 176°F (76 - 80°C)
^ Fully open: 194°F (90°C)
