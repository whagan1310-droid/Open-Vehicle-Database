Sunlight Sensor - Automatic Climate Control

SUNLIGHT SENSOR
The sunlight sensor is a light sensitive, variable resistance diode. The resistance of the diode increases as the intensity of the light increases. Use a digital multimeter (KS-AHM-32-003) to check it.
