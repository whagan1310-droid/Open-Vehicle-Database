Audible Warning Device: Description and Operation

Seat Belt Reminder


With the ignition switch in RUN or START voltage is applied to the seat belt reminder light. When you unbuckle the driver's seat belt, the integrated control unit senses ground at the BLU/RED wire. The seat belt reminder light will come on and stay on until the driver's seat belt is refastened. A beeper will also sound for about 5 seconds.


Ignition Key-In Reminder


When the ignition key switch is closed, ground is provided at the BLU wire of the integrated control unit. When you open the driver's door, ground is provided at the GRN/BLU wire of the integrated control unit and the beeper sounds.


Lights-on Reminder


Voltage is applied at all times to the taillight relay When the headlight switch is in PARK or HEAD, ground is applied to the taillight relay and the relay contacts close. Voltage is then applied to the integrated control unit through the relay and the RED/BLK wire. When you open the driver's door, the integrated control unit senses ground at the GRN/BLU wire of C502. If voltage is present at fuse 1 and ground is present at the GRN/BLU wire, the lights-on chime module sounds.

On models without security, voltage is applied to the integrated control unit when the headlight switch is in PARK or HEAD.
