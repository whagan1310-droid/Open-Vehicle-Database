Wheel Bearing: Adjustments


Rear Wheel Bearing

Wheel bearings and hub on these models are an integral assembly and bearings cannot be adjusted. If hub endplay exceeds 0.002 inch with spindle nut properly tightened, hub and bearing assembly should be replaced.
