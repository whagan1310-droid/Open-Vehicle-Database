Clearing Trouble Codes

ABS LAMP RESET PROCEDURE

- If all repairs have been completed, disconnect the ABS B2 (15 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box for at least three (3) seconds to erase the ABS control unit's memory.

NOTE: The ABS control unit's memory will be erased if the connector is disconnected from the ABS control unit or the ABS control unit is removed from the body.
