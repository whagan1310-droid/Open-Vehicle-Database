Indicator Light Troubleshooting



SRS Indicator Light Troubleshooting

Possible conditions:

1. SRS light does not come on at all - refer to Indicator Does Not Light procedure.

2. SRS light stays on continuously - refer to Indicator Stays on Continuously procedure.

3. SRS light comes on in combination with a failure of another electrical system (brake system light, engine check light, etc.). Check for damage/corrosion at the under-dash fuse box connector.

NOTES:
- Before starting the applicable troubleshooting, check the condition of all SRS connectors and ground points.
- If the fault is not found after completing the applicable troubleshooting, substitute a known-good SRS unit and check whether the light indication goes away. If it does, the original SRS unit must be faulty; replace it.
