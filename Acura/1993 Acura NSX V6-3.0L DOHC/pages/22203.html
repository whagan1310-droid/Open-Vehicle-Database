Tail Lamp: Description and Operation

With the combination light switch in PARK or HEAD, battery voltage is supplied through fuses 38 and 39 to the taillights, the rear side marker lights and the license lights and the lights come on. The lights are grounded at G551.

See Headlight Switch details for the application of battery voltage to the fuses.
