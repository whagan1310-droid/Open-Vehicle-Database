DTC 41











Self-diagnosis Check Engine light indicates code 41: A problem in the Left Oxygen (02) Sensor Heater circuit.
The oxygen sensors have internal heaters. The heaters stabilize the sensors' output. The oxygen sensors are installed in the exhaust manifolds.
