Starting Air Valve: Description and Operation

Air Boost Valve Cutaway:




Air Boost Valve:





The Air Boost Valve, located on the front of the intake manifold, supplies additional air to the intake manifold when cranking to ease engine starting. The additional air flows from a port in front of the throttle valve through the Air Boost Valve and into the intake manifold. When the engine has started, manifold vacuum closes the Air Boost Valve stopping the flow of the additional air.
