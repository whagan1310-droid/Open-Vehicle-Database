DTC 6











Self-diagnosis Check Engine light indicates code 6: A problem in the Coolant Temperature (TW) Sensor circuit.
The TW sensor is a temperature dependant resistor (thermistor). The resistance of the thermistor decreases as the coolant temperature increases as shown.
