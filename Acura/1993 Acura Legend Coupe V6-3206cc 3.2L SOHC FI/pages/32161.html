Heated Element: Description and Operation

With the ignition switch in RUN, voltage is applied through fuse 19 to the heated mirror switch. When you turn the switch ON, voltage is provided to the indicator, causing it to go on. Voltage is also provided to the heating elements in the mirrors, and the elements heat up to remove fog from the mirrors.
