DTC 10











Self-diagnosis Check Engine light indicates code 10: A problem in the Intake Air Temperature (TA) Sensor circuit.
The TA sensor is a temperature dependent resistor (thermistor). The resistance of the thermistor decreases as the intake air temperature increases as shown.
