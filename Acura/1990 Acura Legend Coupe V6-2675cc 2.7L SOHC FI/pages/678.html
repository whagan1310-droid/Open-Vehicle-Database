Idle Speed Too High When Warm

CHECK:

1. Hoses and connections
2. Fast idle valve
3. Electronic Air Control Valve
4. Power steering oil pressure switch
5. Throttle body
6. Throttle cable

NOTE: Sub-systems are ordered according to greatest likelihood of failure and should be checked in the order that they are listed.
