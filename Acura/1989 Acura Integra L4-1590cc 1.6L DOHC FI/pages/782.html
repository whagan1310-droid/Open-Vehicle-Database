Pressure Tests

PRESSURE TESTING




CAUTION: Before testing. he sure transmission is filled to proper level.

NOTE:
^ Stop engine when attaching hoses for pressure tests.

Torque hose fitting to 18 Nm (1.8 kg-m, 12 lb.ft).

^ Do not reuse aluminum washers.


Line Pressure Measurement








^ Set the parking brake securely.
^ Jack up the front of the car and support it with a jack stands.
Run the engine at 2,000 rpm.


Clutch Pressure Measurement








^ Set the parking brake securely and block the rear wheels.
^ Jack up the front of the car and support it with lack stands.
Run the engine at 2,000 rpm.



Throttle Pressure Measurement








^ Set the parking brake securely and block the wheels.
^ Run the engine at 1 ,OOO rpm.
^ Disconnect the throttle control cable from the throttle lever.


Governor Pressure Measurement









^ Set the parking brake securely and block the rear wheels.
^ Jack up the front of the car and support it with jack stands.
^ Run vehicle at (60 km/h) 37.5 mph.
