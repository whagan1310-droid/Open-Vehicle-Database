Wheel Hub: Service Precautions

NOTE:
- Non-genuine wheel weights may corrode and damage the aluminum wheels.
- On the aluminum wheels, remove the center cap from the inside of the wheel after removing the wheel.
- Before installing the brake disc, clean the mating surfaces of the rear hub and inside of the brake disc.
- Before installing the wheel, clean the mating surfaces of the brake disc and inside of the wheel.
