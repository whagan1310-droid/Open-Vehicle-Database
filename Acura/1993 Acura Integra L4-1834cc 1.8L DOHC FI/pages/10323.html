Cranking Signal: Description and Operation


PURPOSE
This signals the Engine Control Module (ECM) whenever the engine is cranking.

OPERATION
The starter signal is generated by the ignition switch whenever it is in the start position. The 12 volt signal informs the control module the engine is being started.

PURPOSE
The ECM uses the starter signal for fuel and timing control at engine start-up.
