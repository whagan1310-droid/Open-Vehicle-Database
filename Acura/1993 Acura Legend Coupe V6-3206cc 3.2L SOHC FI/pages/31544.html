Trunk / Liftgate Latch: Description and Operation


Through fuse 44, battery voltage is supplied at all times to the trunk opener main switch. When the main switch and the trunk opener switch are closed, battery voltage is applied to the trunk release solenoid, and the energized solenoid releases the trunk latch. The solenoid is grounded at G451.
