Crank/CYL Sensor





REMOVAL:

1. Remove the cruise control actuator.
2. Remove the front upper timing belt cover.
3. Remove the timing belt.
4. Remove three bolts and detach the pulley.
5. Remove four bolts and remove the front upper cover back plate.





INSTALLATION:

1. Install the CRANK/CYL sensor on the cylinder head.
2. Align the cam pulley pin with the camshaft hole to install the cam pulley.
