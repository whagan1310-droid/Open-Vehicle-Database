DTC 4

Recirculation Control Motor
Self-diagnosis A/C LED light indicates code 4: A problem in the recirculation control motor circuit. Use a digital multimeter (KS-AHM-32-003) to check it.

The recirculation control motor regulates the fresh/recirc door according to output from the heater control AMP.
