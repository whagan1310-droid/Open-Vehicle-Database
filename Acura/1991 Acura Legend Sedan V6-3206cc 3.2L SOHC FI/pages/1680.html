Antitheft and Alarm Systems: Service and Repair

1. Obtain 5-digit theft protection code number from customer.
2. Enter code number prior to disconnecting battery ground cable, removing #56 (7.5A) fuse, or removing radio.
3. Perform required service.
4. Connect battery ground cable.
5. After restoring power and turning radio on, the word "CODE" will be displayed. Enter 5-digit code to restore operation.
