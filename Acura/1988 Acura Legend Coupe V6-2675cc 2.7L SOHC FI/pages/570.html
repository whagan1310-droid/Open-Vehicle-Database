Fuel Supply System

System Description
The fuel supply system consists of a fuel tank, in-tank high pressure fuel pump, main relay, fuel filter, pressure regulator, pressure regulator cut-off solenoid valve, injectors and injector resistor.
This system delivers pressure regulated fuel to the injectors and cuts the fuel delivery when the engine is not running.
