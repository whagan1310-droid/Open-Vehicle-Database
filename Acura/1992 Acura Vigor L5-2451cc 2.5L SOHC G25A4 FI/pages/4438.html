Fuel Pressure Regulator: Adjustments

The Fuel Pressure Regulator is of the non-adjustable type. It is preset at the factory. If the Fuel Pressure Regulator is found to be faulty or out of specification, as addressed in "DIAGNOSIS AND TESTING PROCEDURES", it should be replaced, refer to "COMPONENT REPLACEMENT AND REPAIR PROCEDURES". For problems concerning the fuel pressure regulator cut-off solenoid (if equipped) refer to, "COMPUTERIZED ENGINE CONTROLS".
