Recall 92V109000: Passenger Side Air Bag Defect


THE PASSENGER SIDE AIR BAG ASSEMBLY WAS PRODUCED WITHOUT IGNITER MATERIAL. THE LACK OF IGNITER MATERIAL CAN CAUSE NON-DEPLOYMENT OR SLOW DEPLOYMENT OF THE PASSENGER SIDE AIRBAG IN CASE OF A VEHICLE COLLISION, REDUCING THE OCCUPANT PROTECTION OFFERED BY THE AIR BAG AND SAFETY BELT SYSTEMS. REPLACE THE PASSENGER SIDE AIR BAG.

SYSTEM: AIR BAGS.

VEHICLE DESCRIPTION: PASSENGER CARS WITH PASSENGER SIDE AIR BAGS.

NOTE: ACURA HAS INFORMED NHTSA THAT ALL AFFECTED CARS HAVE BEEN REPAIRED.

1992 ACURA LEGEND
