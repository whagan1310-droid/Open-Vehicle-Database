Muffler - Buzzing Noise


91acura02

March 11, 1991

BULLETIN NO.

91-007


YEAR MODEL VIN APPLICATION
1991 LEGEND See
ALL With A/T VEHICLES AFFECTED


Buzzing Noise From The Muffler (Supersedes 91-007, dated January 28, 1991)

SYMPTOM

The customer may complain about a buzzing noise that is audible around 2,300 rpm. The noise is similar to a loose heat shield, occurs while accelerating under a moderate load, and is most noticeable after the muffler warms up.

PROBABLE CAUSE

A loose separator plate inside the muffler.

VEHICLES AFFECTED

Coupe: All up to VIN: JH4KA8 ... MC000533
Sedan: All up to VIN: JH4KA7 ... MC016579

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Replace the muffler, using the procedure on page 9-4 in the Service Manual, with the new muffler listed under PARTS INFORMATION.

PARTS INFORMATION

Muffler:
Automatic Trans. 18307-SP0-013

Gasket (1): 18303-SP0-003
Self-Locking Nuts (3): 90115-659-003
