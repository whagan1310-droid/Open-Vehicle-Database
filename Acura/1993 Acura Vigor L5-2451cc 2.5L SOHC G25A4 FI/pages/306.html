Cooling Fans Troubleshooting

ONLY ONE FAN OPERATES w/ ENGINE & A/C ON

1. Faulty No. 34, 38, or 50 Fuse.
2. Faulty radiator or condenser fan motor.
3. Faulty radiator or condenser fan relay.
4. Faulty ground.
5. Open circuit, loose or faulty terminals.

FANS DO NOT ROTATE

1. Faulty No. 8 fuse.
2. Faulty coolant temperature switch A.
3. Faulty coolant temperature switch B.
4. Faulty ground.
5. Open circuit, loose or faulty terminals.

FAN TIMER UNIT IMPROPERLY FUNCTIONING

1. Faulty No. 39 or 2 fuse.
2. Faulty coolant temperature switch B.
3. Faulty fan timer unit.
4. Faulty ground.
5. Open circuit, loose or faulty terminals.
