Manifold Pressure/Vacuum Sensor: Service and Repair

WARNING: Perform this operation on a vehicle that has cooled down to prevent burns.

1. Disconnect 3-wire connector from the Manifold Absolute Sensor (MAP) located on the throttle body.
2. Move any vacuum lines, connectors, and switches that are obstructing the sensor.
3. Remove sensor from the throttle body.
4. Install new sensor and secure to throttle body.
5. Move back any vacuum lines, connectors, and switches that were moved during the removal procedure.
6. Connect the 3-wire connector to the MAP sensor.
