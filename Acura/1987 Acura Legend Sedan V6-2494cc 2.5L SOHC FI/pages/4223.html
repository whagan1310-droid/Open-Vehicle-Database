Component Test





Ambient Temperature Sensor
Compare the resistance reading between terminals of the ambient temperature sensor with specifications shown in the graph: should be within specification.





CAUTION: The sensor uses a thermistor which can be damaged it high current is applied to it during testing. Therefore, use a circuit tester with a measuring current of 1 mA or less.
