Fail-Safe Relay Motor Diagnosis and Testing

Fig. 88 Fail-Safe/Motor Relays:





1. Remove fail-safe relay, then check for continuity between relay terminals A and B, Fig. 88. Continuity should not be indicated.
2. Connect 12 volt battery across terminals C and D. Continuity should be indicated between terminals A and B. Replace relay as needed.
3. Remove motor relay, then check for continuity between relay terminals C and D, Fig. 88. Continuity should be indicated.
4. Connect 12 volt battery across terminals C and D. Continuity should be indicated between terminals A and B. Replace relay as needed.
