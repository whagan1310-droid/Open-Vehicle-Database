37500 MI or 60000 KM

Check
    Brakes and Traction Control
      Inspect brake discs, calipers, and pads every 6 months or 7500 miles.
Inspect
    Steering and Suspension
       Inspect suspension components.
    Steering
      Inspect tie rod ends, steering gear box and dust boots.
    Power Steering
      Inspect power steering system and fluid level every 6 months or 7500 miles.
    Headlamp
      Inspect all exterior lights and lighting controls.
    Fuel Supply Line
      Inspect fuel lines and connections.
    Fluids
      Inspect level and condition of all fluids.
    Exhaust System
    Drum Brake System
      Inspect rear brake drums, wheel cylinders, and linings.
    Disc Brake System
      Inspect front and rear disc brake pads, rotors, and calipers.
    Cooling System
      Inspect cooling system hoses and connections.
    Constant Velocity Joint Boot
    Brake Hose/Line
      Inspect brake hoses and lines. (including ABS if applicable)
    Body and Frame
      Inspect vehicle underbody. Lubricate locks and hinges.
Replace
    Oil Filter, Engine
    Engine Oil
Rotate
    Tires
