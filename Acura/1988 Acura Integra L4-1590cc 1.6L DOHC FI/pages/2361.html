A/T - Upshift Flare From 2ND to 3RD Gear

89acura07

YEAR MODEL VIN APPLICATION
1988 - 1989 INTEGRA ALL With A/T
November 27, 1989

BULLETIN NO.

89-031
Upshift Flare from 2nd to 3rd Gear

SYMPTOM

A slight engine flare-up occurs during the 2 - 3 upshift under light throttle. This is most noticeable when the transmission is cold.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

1. Check the throttle control cable adjustment. If necessary, adjust as described in the service manual and test drive the car. If the symptom still occurs proceed to step 2.

2. Overhaul the transmission as described in section 14 of the Service Manual, and replace the 2nd orifice control valve spring with the part listed under PARTS INFORMATION. Pay particular attention to the condition of the 2nd and 3rd clutch packs.

PARTS INFORMATION

2nd orifice control valve spring: P/N 27417-PL1-850

Gasket kit "C": P/N 061C1-PP1-000

NOTE: You will need to order the clutch pack O-rings separately.
O-ring 104 x 2.2: P/N 91303-PC9-004
(four required)

O-ring 41.8 x 2.2: P/N 91302-PL4-003

O-ring 39.8 x 2.2: P/N 91302-PA9-003
(three required)
