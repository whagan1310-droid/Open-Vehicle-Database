Evaporator Removal and Installation

1. Disconnect battery ground cable and discharge A/C system.
2. Disconnect receiver line and suction hose from evaporator, then immediately plug all open fittings.
3. Remove 2 screws to glove box, then 3 screws and 1 bolt and glove box frame.
4. Remove 2 bolts securing headlamp retractor control module.
5. Remove console as follows:
a. Raise parking brake lever, and on manual transmission models remove shift knob.
b. Pry up screw covers from front and rear of brake lever housing, then remove retaining screws.
c. Remove 2 retaining screws from each side of console, then the console assembly.
6. Remove console box.
7. Remove 2 bolts and 3 screws, then console box bracket.
8. Remove bolts securing instrument panel lower bracket to bracket B, then pry instrument panel bracket forward approximately 1/2 inch to provide clearance.
9. Loosen sealing band and 1 upper blower housing bolt and remove 2 lower bolts securing blower housing.
10. Slide blower assembly as far to right as possible, then disconnect drain hose from evaporator lower housing.
11. Remove 2 bolts securing evaporator housing and lower housing, then remove evaporator.
12. Disconnect thermostat lead from connector and clamp on housing, then remove housing assembly from vehicle.
13. Reverse procedure to install.
