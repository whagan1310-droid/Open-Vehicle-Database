Air Bag Systems: Tools and Equipment


To check operation of deployment tool, connect yellow clips to both switch protector handles of tool. Connect tool to a fully charged 12 volt battery. Depress tool deployment button. If green lamp illuminates, tool is satisfactory. If red lamp illuminates, tool is defective. After completing check, disconnect yellow clips and battery.
