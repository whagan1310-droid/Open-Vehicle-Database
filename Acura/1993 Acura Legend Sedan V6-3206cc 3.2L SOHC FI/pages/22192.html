Canister Purge Solenoid: Description and Operation

Purge Cut-Off System Operation:






PURPOSE
The solenoid is used by the ECM to control the evaporative emission system.

LOCATION
Mounted on left fender, next to charcoal canister.

OPERATION
The ECM applies voltage to the solenoid when engine coolant temperature is below 140°F (60°C). When energized the solenoid cuts vacuum to the purge control valve which prevents canister purging.
