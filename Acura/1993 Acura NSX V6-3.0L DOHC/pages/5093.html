Indicators

When the ignition is in RUN or START, battery voltage is supplied from fuse 5 to the indicators in the gauge assembly. The dimming circuits and some of the indicators are grounded at G401. Most of the indicators are turned on by the individual circuit systems. See the circuits listed for additional information about how each indicator works.
