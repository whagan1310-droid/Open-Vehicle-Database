Door Glass - Vertical Scratches

February 13, 1989

BULLETIN NO. 89-004

YEAR ALL

MODEL LEGEND COUPE

VIN APPLICATION ALL up to VIN JC0019405

Vertical Scratches On Door Glass

PROBLEM

The fiber pads on the inside of the door molding are becoming contaminated with dirt and other gritty-type particles. This contamination results in a breakdown of the fibers' composition, and allows the window to make contact with the metal clips also located on the inside of the molding.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Replace the scratched door glass and install a new, redesigned glass molding as listed under PARTS INFORMATION. See the service manual for door glass and molding replacement procedures.

PARTS INFORMATION

RF door molding assembly: P/N 72410-SG0-003
LF door molding assembly: P/N 72450-SG0-003
RF door glass assembly: P/N 73300-SG0-000
LF door glass assembly: P/N 73350-SG0-000

LF door: 824125
Flat rate time: 1.3 hours

LF door: 72450-SG0-003
Defect code: Oil
