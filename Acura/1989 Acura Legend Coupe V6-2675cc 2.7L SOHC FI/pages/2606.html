DTC 8

Code 8 indicates a problem in the TDC sensor or circuit.

1. Remove Alternator Sense fuse in underhood relay box for 10 seconds to reset ECU.
2. Start engine and observe diagnostic LED.

Flashes code 8, proceed to step 4.
Does not flash, proceed to next step.

3. Malfunction is intermittent. Check connections between ECU and distributor. End of test.


TDC Sensor Code 8 Diagnosis:






4. Stop engine and disconnect 2 wire connector at distributor.
5. Measure resistance between WHT/BLU and ORN/BLU wires.

500 - 1000 ohms, proceed to next step.
Not 500 - 1000 ohms, replace TDC sensor. End of test.

6. Check for continuity to ground between WHT/BLU and ORN/BLU wires individually.

Continuity, replace TDC sensor. End of test.
No continuity, proceed to next step.

7. Reconnect distributor connector.
8. Install test harness main harness only. If test harness is not available, carefully backprobe wires at ECU connector.


TDC Sensor Testing:






9. Check resistance between terminals C3 and C4.

500 - 1000 ohms, proceed to step 11.
Not 500 - 1000 ohms, proceed to next step.

10. Repair open in ORN/BLU or WHT/BLU wire between ECU and distributor. End of test.
11. Check for continuity to ground at terminal C3.

Continuity, proceed to step 13.
No continuity, proceed to next step.

12. Replace electronic control unit. End of test.
13. Repair short to ground in ORN/BLU wire between ECU terminal C3 and distributor.
