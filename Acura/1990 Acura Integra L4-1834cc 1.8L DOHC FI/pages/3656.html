A/T Shift Position Signal

A/T Shift Position Signal:






The A/T shift position signal is generated by a shifter console mounted switch. When the transmission is shifted into PARK or NEUTRAL terminal B7 on the ECU is grounded. The ECU uses this signal to sense when the transmission is shifted into a drive gear to compensate engine load.
