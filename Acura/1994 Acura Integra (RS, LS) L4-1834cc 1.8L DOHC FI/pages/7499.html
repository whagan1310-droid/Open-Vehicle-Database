Blower Motor: Service and Repair

1. On models equipped with radio coded theft protection system, refer to Vehicle Damage Warnings for system disarming and arming procedures.
2. On models less A/C, remove wire harness clips from heater duct, then remove four screws and duct.
3. On models with A/C, remove evaporator assembly.
