Automatic Climate Control





REPLACEMENT
1. Remove the dashboard lower panel and disconnect the connector from it. Service and Repair
2. Disconnect the air mix control motor connector and max cool connector.
3. Unhook the holders from the motor rod, then remove its mounting screws and the air mix control motor and max cool motor.
4. Remove the air mix control motor as shown.
