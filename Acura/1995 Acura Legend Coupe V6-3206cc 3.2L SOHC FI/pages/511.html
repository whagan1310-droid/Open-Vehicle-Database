Clearing Diagnostic Trouble Codes




ECM Reset Procedure
1. Turn the ignition switch off.
2. Remove the No. 15 (ACGS) (7.5 A) fuse from the under-dash fuse/relay box for 10 seconds to reset the ECM (Engine Control Module).

NOTE: Disconnecting the No. 15 fuse also cancels the power seat setting.
