Spare Tire - Anchor Bolt Is Too Short

91acura01 January 28, 1991
YEAR MODEL
1991 NSX
VIN APPLICATION BULLETIN NO.
All up to VIN
JH4NA1 ... MT000647 91-009
Longer Spare Tire Bolt

PROBLEM

The spare tire anchor bolt is too shortto fully engage the threaded nut.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Replace the spare tire bolt on all cars within the designated VIN range with the improved part listed under PARTS INFORMATION. Replacement should be done when the car is brought in for service.

PARTS INFORMATION

Bolt: P/N 74240-SL0-020
WARRANTY CLAIM INFORMATION

In warranty: The normal warranty applies.

Out-of-warranty: Any repair performed after warranty expiration may be eligible for goodwill consideration by the District Service Manager. You must request consideration, and get the DSM's decision, before starting work.

Flat rate time: 0.2 hour

Failed P/N: 74240-SL0-020

Defect code: 028

Contention code: B99
