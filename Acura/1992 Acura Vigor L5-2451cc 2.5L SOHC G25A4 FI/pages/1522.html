Failure Mode K: SRS Indicator Wire Harness Short or Open

1. Disconnect battery ground cable, then positive cable.
2. Turn ignition switch Off, then wait at least three minutes to allow capacitor in back-up circuit to discharge, then disconnect airbag connector.
3. Install short connector (red) on airbags and seat pretensioners, install short connector A (Tool No. 07MAZ-SP00200) on drivers airbag cable reel connector, then one on SRS main harness connector (for passenger side airbag). This will prevent any static electricity from triggering seat belt pretensioners.
4. Reconnect battery positive and ground cables.
5. Disconnect SRS main wire harness 4-P connector from cabin wire harness.
6. With ignition switch On, measure voltage between terminal No. 1 and body ground on main harness side of 4-P connector. If indicated voltage is greater than 9.0 volts, proceed to step 10. If voltage is less than 9.0 volts, proceed to step 7.
7. Connect test harness B between SRS main harness 18-P connector.
8. Check for continuity between terminal B11 and body ground, if continuity is indicated, the SRS main harness is shorted, replace harness, then recheck voltages. If continuity is not indicated, proceed to step 9.
9. Check for continuity between test harness B terminal B11 and main wire harness 4-P connector terminal No. 1, if continuity is indicated, replace SRS unit, then recheck voltages. If continuity is not indicated, SRS main harness is open, replace harness or repair car main harness blue wire, then recheck voltage.
10. Connect SRS main harness 4-P connector to main wire harness, and connect SRS main harness 18-P connector to SRS unit. Disconnect dashboard wire harness 16-P connector from gauge assembly, measure voltage between terminal No. 12 and body ground with ignition switch On, if voltage is greater than 9.0 volts, then SRS indicator circuit is faulty, replace gauge assembly and recheck voltages. If voltage is less than 9.0 volts, the dashboard wire harness is faulty, repair open or short in dashboard harness blue wire and recheck voltages.
