Intake Air System





Air Intake System Description
This system supplies air for all engine needs. It consists of the air cleaner, air intake pipe, throttle body, EACV, fast idle valve, throttle control system and intake manifold. A resonator in the air intake pipe provides additional silencing as air is drawn into the system.
