Initial Inspection and Diagnostic Overview


1. Check fuse 16 by operating the windshield wiper.

2. Check G551 by operating the power door locks.

3. Make sure G501 is clean and tight.

4. Check fuses 2, 6, 7 and 8 by visual inspection.

5. Check G301 by observing the clock.
