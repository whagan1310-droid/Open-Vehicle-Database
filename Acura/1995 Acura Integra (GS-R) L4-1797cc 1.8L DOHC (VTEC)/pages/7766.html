Radiator Support: Description and Operation

Description

The front bulkhead is joined to the front wheelhouse and front side frame. It forms the base for the headlights and other parts and maintains the rigidity of the front section of the body. Pay particular attention to twists and parallelism and check mounting of related parts when welding.
