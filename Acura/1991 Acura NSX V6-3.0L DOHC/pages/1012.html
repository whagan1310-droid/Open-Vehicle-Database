Recirculation Control Motor

Recirculation Control Motor
Self-diagnosis indicator light H comes on: Indicates a problem in the recirculation control motor circuit. Use a digital multimeter (KS-AHM32-003) to check it.

The recirculation control motor regulates the fresh/recirc door according to output from the control unit.
