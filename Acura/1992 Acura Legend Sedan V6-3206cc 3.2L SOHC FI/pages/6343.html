Automatic Climate Control

Mode Control Motor


RESISTANCE SPECIFICATIONS
Between No. 3 and 5 terminals approx. 6 K Ohms
Between No. 4 and No. 5 terminals [1]
In VENT Position approx. 1.2 K Ohms
In DEF Position approx. 4.8 K Ohms

[1] Tested while briefly applying power to No. 2 terminal and grounding No. 1 terminal. Also tested in same manner with polarity reversed.
