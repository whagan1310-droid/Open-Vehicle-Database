Air Door Cable: Adjustments

COOL VENT CABLE ADJUSTMENT AND INSTALLATION





1. Slide the Cool Vent control lever to shut.





2. Close the Cool Vent door fully, then connect the end of the Cool Vent cable to the door arm, and snap the cable housing into the clamp.

NOTE: After installation, check that the cable moves smoothly and the door fully opens and closes when operating the control lever.
