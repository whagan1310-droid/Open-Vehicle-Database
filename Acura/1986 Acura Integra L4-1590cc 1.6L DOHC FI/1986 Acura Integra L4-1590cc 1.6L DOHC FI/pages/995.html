Heating and Air Conditioning

TORQUE VALUES

Suction Pipe And Hose (5/8") 23 ftlb (32 Nm)
Discharge Pipe And Hose (1/2") 16 ftlb (22 Nm)
Receiver Pipe And Pipe Joint (3/8") 12 ftlb (17 Nm)
Suction Hose Bolt 22 ftlb (30 Nm)
Discharge Hose Bolt 22 ftlb (30 Nm)
Compressor Mounting Bolts 19 ftlb (26 Nm)
Compressor Clutch Hub Nut 11 - 12.7 ftlb (15 - 17.5 Nm)
Belt Idler Pulley Nut 33 ftlb (45 Nm)

COMPRESSOR DRIVE BELT TENSION

9/32 - 23/64 in (7 - 9 mm) 22 lb (10 kg) force between pulleys.

ADD REFRIGERANT OIL AFTER COMPONENT REPLACEMENT

Condenser 1/3 fl oz (10 cc)
Evaporator 1 fl oz (30 cc)
Line Or Hose 1/3 fl oz (10 cc)
Receiver 1/3 fl oz (10 cc)
Compressor Follow manufacturer instructions
