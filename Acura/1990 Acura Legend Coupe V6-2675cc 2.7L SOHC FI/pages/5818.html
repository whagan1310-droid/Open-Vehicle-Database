Fuel Injection Signals (A/T)

The A/T control unit sends reference signals to the ECU on terminals D3 and D4. They will be referred to as A/T FI signals A and B. These signals inform the ECU of operating conditions within the automatic transaxle relevant to engine load. If for any reason either reference signal fails, the check engine light will be lit and a fault code appropriate to the malfunctioning signal line will be set.
