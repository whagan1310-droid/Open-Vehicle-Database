License Plate Lamp: Description and Operation

With the headlight switch in PARK or HEAD, battery voltage is supplied through fuses 48 and 49 to the taillights, the rear side marker lights, and the license plate lights, and the lights go on. The lights are grounded at G451.


See headlight switch details for the application of battery voltage to the fuses.
