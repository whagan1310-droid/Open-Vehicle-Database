Compressor HVAC: Description and Operation

The air conditioner system is turned on by pressing the A/C switch on the heater control panel. This provides a ground signal to the heater control amplifier.

The heater control amplifier then sends a signal to the engine control module (ECM) through the A/C pressure switch. The switch is closed with normal pressure in the system.

The ECM provides a ground to the A/C compressor clutch relay The relay, which is powered through fuse 8 with the ignition switch in RUN, energizes and the clutch engages.
