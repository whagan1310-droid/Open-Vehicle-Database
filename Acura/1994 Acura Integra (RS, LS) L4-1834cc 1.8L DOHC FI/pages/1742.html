SRS Indicator Light Doesn't Come On

THE SRS INDICATOR DOES NOT LIGHT

CAUTION: Use only a digital multimeter to check the system. If it's not a Honda multimeter, make sure its output is 10 mA (0.01 A) or less when switched to the smallest value in the ohmmeter range. A tester with a higher output could damage the airbag circuit or cause accidental airbag deployment and possible injury.
