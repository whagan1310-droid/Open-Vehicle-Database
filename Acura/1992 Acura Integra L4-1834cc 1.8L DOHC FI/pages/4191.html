Shift Solenoid: Service and Repair





Replacement
1. Remove the mounting bolts and shift control solenoid valve assembly.

NOTE: Be sure to remove or replace the shift control solenoid valves A and B as an assembly.

2. Check the shift control solenoid valve oil passages for dust or dirt and replace as an assembly, if necessary.
3. Clean the mounting surface and oil passages of the shift control solenoid valve assembly and install a new base gasket.
4. Check the connector for rust, dirt or oil and reconnect it securely.
