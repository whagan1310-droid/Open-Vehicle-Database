Door/Trunk Ajar Indicator/Lamp: Description and Operation

The safety indicator light goes onto alert the driver to safety related conditions. The display consists of bulbs arranged to illuminate a graphic display of the car. With the ignition in RUN or START battery voltage is supplied through fuse 13 to the safety indicator circuit and each of the indicators.


Indicator Check


When the ignition is placed in RUN or START, the safety indicator circuit grounds the indicator bulbs for several seconds. The indicators then come on for several seconds to test the display.


Ajar Indicators


When any door switch or trunk latch switch is closed (door or lid open), a ground is provided for the appropriate indicator. The indicator remains on as long as the switch is closed, and goes off when the switch is open (door or lid closed).
