DTC List - Transmission Control








- If a customer describes the symptoms for codes 3, 6, or 11, yet the LED is not blinking, it will be necessary to recreate the symptom by test driving, and then checking the LED with the ignition still ON.
- If the LED displays codes other than those listed above or stays lit continuously, the control unit is faulty.
- Sometimes the S3 indicator light and the Check Engine warning light may come on simultaneously. If so, check the PGM-FI system according to the number of blinks on the PGM-FI ECU self-diagnosing indicator, then reset the memory by removing the Clock fuse in the dash fuse box for more than 10 seconds. Drive the vehicle for several minutes at speed over 30 mph (50 km/h), then recheck the lights.
