Component Test - Radiator and Condenser Fan Controls

Fig. 24 Fan Motor Terminal Identification:






1. Disconnect the 2-P connector from each fan motor.
2. Test the motor by connecting battery power to the A terminal and ground to the B terminal.
3. If the motor fails to run or does not run smoothly, replace it.
