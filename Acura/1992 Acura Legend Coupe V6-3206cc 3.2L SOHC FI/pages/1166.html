Ignition Lock: Service and Repair

1. Disable coded theft protection system as described under Accessories and Optional Equipment / Antitheft and Alarm Systems / Service and Repair.
2. On models equipped with airbag, disarm airbag system as described under Air Bags and Seat Belts / Air Bags (Supplemental Restraint Systems) / Airbag System Disarming.
3. Remove ignition switch as previously described.
4. On Legend models, remove key light case socket and bulb by rotating socket 45°, then remove screw and case from lock assembly, if equipped.
5. On all models, insert ignition key and move switch to ``I'' position.
6. Depress pin and remove lock cylinder from body.
7. Turn ignition key to ``lock'' position and align lock cylinder with body.
8. Turn key almost to ``I'' position and insert lock cylinder until pin contacts body.
9. Turn key to ``I'' position, then depress pin and insert lock cylinder until pin clicks into place.
10. Install ignition switch.
11. Restore coded theft protection system and rearm airbag.
