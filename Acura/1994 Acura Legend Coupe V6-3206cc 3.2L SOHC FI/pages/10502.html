Set/Resume/Cancel Switch

1. On models equipped with radio coded theft protection system, refer to Vehicle Damage Warnings for system disarming and arming procedures.
2. Remove dashboard lower panel.
3. Disconnect cable reel harness.
4. Remove steering column lower cover and disconnect cable reel connector. Connect SRS test harness C to cable reel connector.

Fig. 26 Column Harness:





5. Check for continuity between terminals of test harness connector in each switch position as shown in Fig. 26.
6. If there is continuity in each switch position, set/resume/cancel switch is functional. If there is no continuity in one or some switch positions, replace switch.

Fig. 27 Set/Resume/Cancel Switch:





7. Remove cover from set/resume/cancel switch, then remove two screws and switch from steering wheel. Check for continuity in each switch position as shown in Fig. 27.
8. If there is no continuity in one or some switch positions, replace switch. If there is continuity in each switch position, replace cable reel.
9. After service, reactivate radio as outlined under Vehicle Damage Warnings.
