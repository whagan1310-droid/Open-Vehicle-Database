Removal and Installation

1. Raise and support vehicle.
2. Remove front wheels.
3. Drain differential oil.
4. Raise locking tab on spindle nut and loosen it.
5. Remove damper fork nut, pinch bolt and fork.
6. Loosen, but do not remove, lower arm ball joint nut.
7. Support lower arm, then separate lower ball joint from lower arm.
8. On left driveshaft, proceed as follows:
a. Pry driveshaft out with a screwdriver to force set ring at driveshaft end past groove.
b. Pull inboard joint and remove driveshaft assembly.
9. On right driveshaft, remove driveshaft from bearing support by tapping inboard joint of driveshaft with a plastic hammer.
10. On both driveshafts, remove spindle nut.
11. Pull knuckle outward and remove driveshaft outboard joint from front hub using a plastic hammer.
12. Reverse procedure to install.
