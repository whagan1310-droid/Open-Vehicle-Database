Audio - High Frequency Hum or Silence in Dsp Mode


91acura04

May 13, 1991

BULLETIN NO

91-027


YEAR MODEL VIN APPLICATION
1992 VIGOR GS TO JH4CC2.5.NC003378


High Frequency Hum or Silence in DSP Mode


SYMPTOM

A high-pitched hum or no sound at all when operating the audio system in the DSP mode. This may be an intermittent symptom.


PROBABLE CAUSE

The DSP circuit is affected by heat. The DSP function returns to normal when the internal temperature falls to below 140 degrees F, or when the DSP function is turned off.


CORRECTIVE ACTION

Replace the audio system according to the procedures in Service Bulletin 87-015.


PARTS INFORMATION

Call Panasonic at 1-800-423-8150 for a replacement. Have the following information ready when you call:

^ Dealer name and number

^ VIN

^ Mileage

^ Dealer contact person

^ Retail delivery date of the car

^ Customer's name
