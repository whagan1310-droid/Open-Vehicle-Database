Automatic Climate Control

REPLACEMENT
1. Remove the glove box lower panel and disconnect the connector from it. Service and Repair
2. Disconnect the air mix control motor connector.





3. Remove the air mix control motor as shown.
4. Install the air mix control motor in the reverse order of removal. After installation, make sure the air mix control motor operates smoothly.
