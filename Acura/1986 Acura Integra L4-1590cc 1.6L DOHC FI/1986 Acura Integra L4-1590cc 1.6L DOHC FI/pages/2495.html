Instrument Cluster / Carrier: Service and Repair

Fig. 14 Instrument panel removal. Integra:





1. Disconnect battery ground cable, then lower steering column as necessary
2. Remove dash panel switches by pushing outward, then disconnect electrical connectors.
3. Remove instrument panel caps and attaching screws, then the instrument panel, Fig. 14.
4. Remove instrument cluster attaching screws, then pull cluster away from dash.
5. Disconnect speedometer cable and electrical connector from instrument cluster, then remove the cluster from vehicle.
6. Reverse procedure to install.
