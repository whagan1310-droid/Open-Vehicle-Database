Fuel Pressure Regulator: Adjustments

The Fuel Pressure Regulator is of the non-adjustable type. It is preset at the factory. If the Fuel Pressure Regulator is found to be faulty or out of specification, as addressed in "Fuel Delivery and Air Induction/Testing and Inspection/Procedures", it should be replaced. Refer to "Fuel Pressure Regulator/Service and Repair Procedures" for replacement instructions.
