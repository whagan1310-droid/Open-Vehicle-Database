DTC 1


The Malfunction Indicator Lamp indicates Diagnostic Trouble Code 1: A problem in the Front Heated Oxygen Sensor circuit.





The Heated Oxygen Sensors, detect the oxygen content in the exhaust gas, and input the engine control module. In operation, the engine control module receives the signals from the sensors and varies the duration during which fuel is injected. The Heated Oxygen Sensors are combined with heaters. The heaters stabilize the sensors output. The Heated Oxygen Sensors are installed in the exhaust manifolds.
