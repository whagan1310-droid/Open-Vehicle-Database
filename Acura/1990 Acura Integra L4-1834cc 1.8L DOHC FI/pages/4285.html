ABS Light: Testing and Inspection

Warning Light Circuit:
1. The dash warning light, does not go on when the ignition switch is turned on. Check the following items. If they are OK, check the control unit connectors. If not loose or disconnected, install a new control unit and recheck:

- Blown dash warning light bulb.

- Open circuit in YEL lead between No.23 fuse and combination meter.

- Open circuit in BLU/RED lead between combination meter and control unit.

- Loose component grounding of the control unit to the body.

2. The dash warning light remains ON or after the engine is started, however the LED on the control unit does not blink any code or sub-code, check for the following:

- Loose or poor connection of the wire harness at the control unit.

- Faulty ALB B2 fuse.

- Open circuit in WHT lead between ALB B2 fuse and control unit.

- Open circuit in BLK/YEL lead between fuse No.17 and fail safe relay(s).

- Open or short circuit in the GRN lead between fail safe relay(s).

- Short circuit in BLU/RED lead between combination meter and control unit.

- Open circuit in WHT/BLU lead between alternator and control unit.

If the problem is not found substitute a known good control unit and recheck whether the warning light remains ON.
