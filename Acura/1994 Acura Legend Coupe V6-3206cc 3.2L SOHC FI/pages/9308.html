High Pressure Safety Valve HVAC: Service and Repair





1. Remove the relief valve and O-ring. Don't let any compressor oil run out.
2. Clean off the mating surface.
3. Replace O-ring with new one at the relief valve, and apply a thin coat of refrigerant oil (ND-OIL 8: PIN 38899-PR7-AO1) before installing it.

NOTE:
- Do not return the oil to the container once dispensed and never mix with other refrigerant oils to avoid contamination.
- Immediately after using the oil, replace the cap on the container and seal it to avoid moisture absorption.
- Do not spill the refrigerant oil on the car; it may damage the paint; if the refrigerant oil contacts the paint, wash it off immediately.

4. Install and tighten the relief valve. Torque to 13.5 Nm (9.8 lb-ft)
5. Charge the system and check for leaks, then push the cap into the relief valve.
