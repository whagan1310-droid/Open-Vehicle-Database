Disassemble

1. Remove the Transmission Housing. Service and Repair
2. Inspect clearance of the Reverse Change Holder/Reverse Idler Gear. Testing and Inspection
3. Remove the Reverse Change Holder/Reverse Idler Gear. Change Holder Assembly
4. Inspect clearance of the Change Holder Assembly. Service and Repair
5. Remove the Change Holder Assembly. Service and Repair
6. Remove the Mainshaft, Countershaft, Shift Fork. Service and Repair
7. Remove Shift Rod. Service and Repair
8. Inspect the Synchro Sleeve/Synchro Hub. Testing and Inspection
9. Inspect the Synchro Ring/Gear. Testing and Inspection
