Fuel Tank Pressure Release Valve: Specifications


CONSTANT VACUUM CONTROL VALVE
At outlet VACUUM 200 mmHg (8 in.Hg)

ENGINE OPERATING TEMPERATURE
Minimum 80 to 90°C (180 to 190°F)

FAST IDLE VALVE
Open below 30°C (86°F)

PURGE SOLENOID
On above 70°C (158°F)

TWO-WAY VALVE:
Open VACUUM 5 to 15 mmHg (.2 to .6 in.Hg)
Open PRESSURE 10 to 35 mmHg (0.4 to 1.4 in.Hg)
