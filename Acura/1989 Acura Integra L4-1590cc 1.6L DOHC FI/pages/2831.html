Dashpot: Service and Repair

Throttle Body:





1. Remove the two (2) Dashpot mounting screws located on top of the throttle body assembly.
2. Disconnect Dashpot diaphragm rod.
3. Reconnect Dashpot diaphragm rod to throttle valve actuator.
4. Reinstall Dashpot mounting screws.
5. Retest Dashpot operation and idle speed, refer to "DIAGNOSIS AND TESTING PROCEDURES".
