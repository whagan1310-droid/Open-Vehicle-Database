Difficult to Start Cold

CHECK:

1. Engine Coolant Temperature Sensor

2. Fast Idle Thermo Valve

3. Starting Air Valve

4. TDC/CYP Position Sensor

5. Manifold Absolute Pressure Sensor

6. Fuel Pressure Regulator

7. Barometric Pressure Sensor

8. Fuel Pressure Regulator Cut-off Solenoid Valve [1]

[1] Refer to "COMPUTERS AND CONTROL SYSTEMS"

NOTE: After each symptom, the sub-system that could be the source of the symptom are listed and should be checked in the order they are listed, beginning with #1.
