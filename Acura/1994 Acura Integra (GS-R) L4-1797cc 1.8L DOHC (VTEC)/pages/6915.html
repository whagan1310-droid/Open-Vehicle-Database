Brake Fluid Leak
(Supersedes 94-013, dated October 4, 1994)

SYMPTOM

The brake fluid level is low in the ABS reservoir, and fluid appears to be leaking from under the label on the ABS accumulator. [NEW]

PROBABLE CAUSE

The ABS accumulator internal O-ring is damaged, allowing brake fluid to leak out.

DIAGNOSIS [NEW]

Remove the label from the ABS accumulator.

^ If the hole behind the label is wet with brake fluid, continue to CORRECTIVE ACTION.

^ If the hole is not wet, clean the accumulator and use powder to locate the leak.

PARTS INFORMATION
