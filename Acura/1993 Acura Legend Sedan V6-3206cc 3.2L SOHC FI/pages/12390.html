Ignition Control Module: Service and Repair

Ignition Control Module (ICM):






1. Disconnect the 8-P and 6-P connectors from the igniter unit. The igniter unit is mounted on the left strut tower.
2. Remove the 2 mounting bolts from the igniter unit.
3. Install new igniter unit and reconnect connectors.
