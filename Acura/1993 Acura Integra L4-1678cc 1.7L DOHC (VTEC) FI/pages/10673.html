Ignition Cable: Description and Operation


Spark Plug Wire Inspection:





PURPOSE
The spark plug wires connect the terminals in the distributor cap to the spark plugs, allowing high voltage to flow to the spark plugs.

CONSTRUCTION
The wires have an internal resistance to suppress radio interference.
