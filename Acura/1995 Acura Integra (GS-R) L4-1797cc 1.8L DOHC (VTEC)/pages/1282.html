Wheel Sensor Signal Confirmation

1. Disconnect 6 pin inspection connector from connector cover on crossmember and connect 6 pin inspection connector to ABS connector.
2. Raise vehicle so four wheels are off ground and supported on safety stands. Turn ignition switch On and set mode switch to 0.
3. With transmission in neutral, rotate each wheel by hand. Ensure each monitor on checker blinks as wheel rotates. Rotating a wheel too slowly will produce only a weak blink. In bright sunlight lights may be difficult to see. If necessary, perform these tests in shaded area. If front wheels are not rotating fast by hand, it may be necessary to start engine, slowly accelerate and decelerate front wheels.
4. If any monitor light fails to blink, check sensor for air gap and wiring.
