120000 MI or 192000 KM

Inspect/Adjust
    Parking Brake System
    Drive Belt
      Tension adjustment only.
Clean
    Antenna
      Clean antenna mast
Check
    Brakes and Traction Control
Inspect
    Steering
      Inspect tie rod ends, steering gear box and dust boots.
    Power Steering
      Inspect every 7500 miles or 6 months if operating in dusty conditions, severe cold weather, or in areas with road salt or other corrosive materials.
    Positive Crankcase Ventilation
      Inspect PCV system.
    Ignition Rotor
    Ignition Cable
    Headlamp
      Inspect all exterior lights and lighting controls.
    Fuel Supply Line
      Inspect fuel lines and connections.
    Fluids
      Inspect level and condition of all fluids.
    Exhaust System
    Drum Brake System
      Inspect rear brake drums, wheel cylinders, and linings.
    Distributor Cap
    Disc Brake System
      Inspect front and rear disc brake pads, rotors, and calipers.
    Cooling System
      Inspect cooling system hoses and connections.
    Brake Hose/Line
      Inspect brake hoses and lines. (including ABS if applicable)
    Body and Frame
      Inspect vehicle underbody. Lubricate locks and hinges. Inspect vehicle underbody, lubricate hinges and locks.
Replace
    Spark Plug
      Non-platinum spark plugs only.
    Oil Filter, Engine
    Engine Oil
    Air Filter Element
Rotate
    Tires
