Trunk Lamp: Description and Operation

Battery voltage is supplied at all times through fuse 34 to the driver's and passenger's door courtesy lights, ceiling light and trunk light.


Courtesy Lights


When the driver's door is opened, its door switch closes, providing a ground for the driver's door courtesy light, and the light comes on. The passenger's door courtesy light works the same way.

When either door is opened and the switch on the ceiling light is in the door mode, the ceiling lights will come on.


Ceiling Light


The ceiling light has three modes: off, door, and on. When the ceiling light mode switch is in OFF, the ceiling lights cannot be turned on by any of the other switches.

When the ceiling light mode switch is in DOOR, and you open one of the doors, the door switch closes, providing a ground for the ceiling light and the courtesy light in that door. The diodes in the gauge assembly prevent the courtesy light in the unopened door from coming on.

With the ceiling light mode switch in ON, both bulbs are case grounded by the light housing. Both bulbs go on regardless of the individual door switch positions.


Trunk Light


When you open the trunk, the trunk latch switch contacts close, grounding the trunk light bulb, and the trunk light comes on.
