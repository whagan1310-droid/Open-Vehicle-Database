Manual Transmission

On models equipped with airbag system, refer to Technician Safety Information for system disarming and arming procedures.

1. On models equipped with radio coded theft protection system, refer to Vehicle Damage Warnings for system disarming and arming procedures.
2. Remove ignition switch as described under Ignition Switch, Replace.
3. Insert ignition key and move switch to l position.
4. Depress pin and remove lock cylinder from body.
5. Turn ignition key to lock position and align lock cylinder with body.
6. Turn key almost to I position and insert lock cylinder until pin contacts body.
7. Turn key to I position, then depress pin and insert lock cylinder until pin clicks into place.
8. Install ignition switch.
9. On models equipped with airbag system, refer to Technician Safety Information for system disarming and arming procedures.
10. On models equipped with radio coded theft protection system, refer to Vehicle Damage Warnings for system disarming and arming procedures.
