Clutch Pedal - ``Notchy'' Feeling

90acura03
YEAR MODEL VIN APPLICATION
1990 INTEGRA ALL
October 22, 1990

BULLETIN NO

90-021
"Notchy" Feeling in lntegra Clutch Pedal


SYMPTOM

A "notchy" feeling in the clutch pedal near its fully-depressed position as you depress or release the pedal.


PROBABLE CAUSE

Friction between the clutch cable and its housing.


CORRECTIVE ACTION

Replace the clutch cable with the one listed under PARTS INFORMATION. Adjust it to 4 - 5 mm (3/16") free play at the release arm.


PARTS INFORMATION

Clutch cable: P/N 22910-SK7-A02


WARRANTY CLAIM INFORMATION

In warranty: The normal warranty applies.

Out-of-warranty: Any repair performed after warranty expiration may be eligible for goodwill consideration by the District Service Manager. You must request consideration, and get the DSM's decision, before starting work.
