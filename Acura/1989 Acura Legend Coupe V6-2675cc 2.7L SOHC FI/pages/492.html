Engine Won't Start

Engine Won't Start
Check:

1. Fuel Supply
- Fuel Pump
- Main Relay
- Fuel Filter
- Injector Resistor
2. ECU (0)
3. Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP) Sensor (3) or (5)
3. Coolant Temperature (TW) Sensor (6)
3. Atmospheric Pressure (PA) Sensor (13)
3. Ignition Output Signal (15)

NOTE: After each symptom, the system that could be the source of the symptom are listed and should be checked in the order they are listed, beginning with #1. Items listed with the same number are equally likely to be source of the problem. In this case, the sequence of testing is not important.

NOTE: Numbers in parentheses indicate Self-diagnostic codes which may be present.
