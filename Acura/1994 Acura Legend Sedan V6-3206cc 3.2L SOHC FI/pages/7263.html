Fillpipe Restrictor: Description and Operation

PURPOSE
The Fillpipe Restrictor prevents the introduction of fuel containing lead into the fuel tank by preventing large diameter leaded fuel nozzles from being placed into the filler neck.

OPERATION
A hole is provided near the opening of the neck that is of a diameter that allows only an unleaded nozzle to enter the hole in the neck. A flapper door allows fuel to enter the filler neck and tank only when the door is pushed open by the end of the nozzles.
