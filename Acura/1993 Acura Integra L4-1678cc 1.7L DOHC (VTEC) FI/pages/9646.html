Solenoid Valve

VTEC Solenoid Valve Inspection:






REMOVAL
1. Disconnect the 1-wire connector from the solenoid valve.
2. Remove the 3 bolts securing the solenoid valve to the solenoid valve assembly.
3. Remove the old filter.


INSTALLATION
4. Install a new filter.
5. Align the solenoid valve and install the 3 bolts.
6. Torque the 3 bolts to 12 Nm (8.7 ft lb).
7. Re-connect the 1-wire connector.
