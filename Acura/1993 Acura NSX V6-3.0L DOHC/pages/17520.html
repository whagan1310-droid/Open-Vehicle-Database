Cigarette Lighter: Description and Operation

Battery voltage is applied at all times through fuse 15 to the normally open contacts of the cigarette lighter relay. With the ignition in ACC or RUN, battery voltage is applied through fuse 8 to the coil of the cigarette lighter relay. The coil is grounded at G4O1. The relay energizes, applying battery voltage to the cigarette lighter, When you push in the lighter, its element completes the circuit to ground (G401) and the element heats up. When the element becomes sufficiently heated, it is spring-released and the circuit opens.
