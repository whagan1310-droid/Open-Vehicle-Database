Fuel Shut-off Solenoid: Description and Operation

The fuel cut-off valve prevents liquid fuel from entering the evaporative loss control system in the event of a vehicle roll-over or when the fuel tank is over-filled. It is located on the top of the fuel tank. The fuel cut-off valve contains a liquid-vapor separator and a check ball assembly. For additional information refer to EMISSION CONTROLS.
