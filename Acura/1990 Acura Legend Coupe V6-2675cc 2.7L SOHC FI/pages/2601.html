System Specifications

RESISTANCE SPECIFICATIONS

Between No. 3 and 5 terminals approx. 4 K ohms
Between No. 4 and 5 terminals [1]
At COOL Position approx. 1.2 K ohms
At HOT Position approx. 4.0 K ohms

[1] Tested while briefly connecting the battery power to the No. 2 terminal and grounding to the No. 1 terminal. Also checked with battery polarity reversed.
