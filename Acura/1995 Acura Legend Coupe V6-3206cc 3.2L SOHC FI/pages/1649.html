Compressor





The compressor is a Nippondenso piston type for HFC-134a (R-134a). A revolving inclined disc drives the surrounding 10 reciprocating pistons. As the inclined disc revolved, it pushes the pistons, protected by a ceramic shoe, thus compressing the refrigerant.
