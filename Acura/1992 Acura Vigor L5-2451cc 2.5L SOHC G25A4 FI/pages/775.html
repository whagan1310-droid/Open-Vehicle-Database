Ignition System: Specifications

IGNITION COIL RESISTANCE:
Primary [1] 0.3 - 0.5 ohms
Secondary [2] 10,800 - 16,200 ohms
Resistance between the B and D terminals 2,090 - 2,310

IGNITION TIMING:
Manual [3] 15~ +/- 2~
Automatic [4] 15~ +/- 2~

SPARK PLUG WIRES [5] 25K ohms max.

[1] Between the A and D terminals.
[2] Between the A and secondary winding terminals.
[3] BTDC (RED) at 700 +/- 50 rpm in neutral.
[4] BTDC (RED) at 700 +/- 50 rpm in neutral.
[5] Test at 70~F (20~C).
