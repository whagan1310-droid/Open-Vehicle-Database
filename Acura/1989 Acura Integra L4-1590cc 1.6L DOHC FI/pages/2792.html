Alternator FR Signal

Alternator Control:





The alternator FR signal is a field report signal. It tells the ECU that a charging condition exists. The ECU knows the amount of amperage required by the engine by monitoring the electrical load detector (ELD) and modifies the operation of the voltage regulator based on engine speed and temperature as well as the input from some of the other sensors. The information received allows the ECU to lessen the electrical and physical load placed on the engine by modifying the alternator output voltage. This operation offers improved economy as well as increased idle smoothness.
