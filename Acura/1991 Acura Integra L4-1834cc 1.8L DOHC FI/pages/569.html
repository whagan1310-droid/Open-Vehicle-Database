Fuel Pressure: Specifications

FAST IDLE VALVE
Operating temp. (coolant) Below 86 degrees F (30 degrees C)

FUEL PRESSURE REGULATOR
Pressure at idle 37 - 44 psi (255 - 304 kpa)

FUEL PUMP
System operating pressure at idle 38 psi (260 kpa)
Displacement 230 cc minimum in 10 seconds
Relief valve opening pressure 64 - 85 psi (441 - 588 kpa)
