Accelerator Pedal: Testing and Inspection

CAUTION: Before testing Accelerator pedal be sure engine is turned "OFF" and is not running.

The Accelerator Pedal should move smoothly throughout its full range of movement. If any binding is felt, check for obstructions above or around pedal assembly. Also check for wires that may be hanging down from dash area. Check to be sure that carpeting or floor mats do not restrict pedal from reaching full travel.
