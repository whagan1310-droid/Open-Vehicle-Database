Vent Door Control Motor

Vent Door Control Motor
Self-diagnosis indicator light comes on: Indicates a problem in the vent door control motor circuit. Use a digital multimeter (KS-AHM32-003) to check it.

The vent door control motor regulates the vent door according to output from the control unit.
