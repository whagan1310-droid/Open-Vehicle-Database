Front

Fig. 2 Exploded View Of Front Disc Brake Assembly:





1. Raise and support front of vehicle and remove wheels.
2. Remove caliper pin bolt, then pivot caliper up away from pads.
3. Remove brake pad shims and the pads, Fig. 2.
4. Clean caliper and inspect for excessive wear or damage.
5. Install pad retainers, then the pad shims and pads, Fig. 2.
6. Back out bleed screw and push in piston to allow caliper to fit over pads, then tighten bleed screw.
7. Pivot caliper into position, then install pin bolt and torque to specifications.
