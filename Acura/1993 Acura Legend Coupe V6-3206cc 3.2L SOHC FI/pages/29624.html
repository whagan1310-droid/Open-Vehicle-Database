Antenna: Description and Operation

With the ignition switch in ACC or RUN, voltage is applied through fuse 4 to the radio. Voltage is applied at all times through fuse 56 to the power antenna relay.


When you turn the radio on, the relay coil is energized and the contacts close. Voltage is applied to the power antenna motor which drives the antenna up to its fully extended position.


When you turn the radio off, the relay contacts open. The polarity of the voltage applied to the motor is now reversed, and the antenna motor retracts the antenna. A mechanical switch controlled by the motor turns the motor off when the antenna reaches maximum height or full retraction.


On models equipped with a trunk-mounted spoiler, a mast antenna retractor relay is used to lower the antenna as soon as you open the trunk lid. Voltage is applied at all times to the coil of the relay. When you open the trunk lid, the coil is grounded, opening the normally closed contacts of the relay. This removes power from the power antenna relay, and the motor then retracts the antenna.
