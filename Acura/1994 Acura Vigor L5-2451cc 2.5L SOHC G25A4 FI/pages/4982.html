Ignition Coil: Specifications


Between the A and D terminals 0.3 - 0.5 ohms
Between the A and secondary terminals 10.8 - 16.2 k ohms
Between the B and D terminals 2.0 - 2.3 k ohms
Between the A and C terminals continuity

NOTE: Resistance will vary with the coil temperature; specifications are at 20 °C (68 °F).
