Manual Heating and Air Conditioning

1. Remove the glove box lower panel. Service and Repair
2. Disconnect the recirculation control motor connector.





3. Remove the recirculation control motor mounting screws and the recirculation control motor.
4. Install the recirculation control motor in the reverse order of removal. After installation, make sure the recirculation control motor operates smoothly.
