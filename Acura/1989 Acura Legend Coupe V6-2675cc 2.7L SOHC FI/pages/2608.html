DTC 10

Code 10 indicates a problem with the intake air temperature (TA) sensor or circuit.

1. Remove Alternator Sense fuse in underhood relay box for 10 seconds to reset ECU.
2. Turn ignition on and observe diagnostic LED.

Flashes code 10, proceed to step 4.
Does not flash, proceed to next step.

3. Malfunction is intermittent. Check connections at TA sensor and ECU.


Air Temperature (TA) Sensor Code 10 Diagnosis:






4. Disconnect TA sensor connection.
5. Measure resistance across TA sensor terminals.

1 - 4 ohms, proceed to next step.
Not 1 - 4 ohms, replace TA sensor. End of test.

6. With ignition on, measure voltage between RED/BLK wire in connector and ground.

5 volts, proceed to next step.
Not 5 volts, proceed to step 9.

7. Check voltage between TA sensor harness terminals.

5 volts, replace electronic control unit. End of test.
Not 5 volts, proceed to next step.

8. Repair open GRN/WHT wire between ECU terminal C14 and TA sensor.
9. Turn ignition off.
10. Install test harness between ECU and connector. If test harness is not available, carefully backprobe wires at ECU connector.
11. Turn ignition on.


Air Temperature (TA) Sensor Testing:






12. Measure voltage between terminals C5 and C14.

5 volts, proceed to next step.
Not 5 volts, proceed to step 14.

13. Repair open RED/BLK wire between ECU terminal C5 ant TA sensor. End of test.
14. Remove ECU test connector C from main harness only.
15. Check voltage between terminals C5 and C14.

5 volts, proceed to next step.
Not 5 volts, replace electronic control unit. End of test.

16. Repair short in RED/BLK wire between ECU terminal C5 and TA sensor.
