Indicator Light Troubleshooting




Indicator Light Troubleshooting

Possible conditions:

1. Warning light does not come on at all - refer to the Indicator Does Not Light.

2. Warning light stays on constantly - refer to "Indicator Stays On Continuously".

3. Warning light comes on in combination with a failure of another electrical system (brake warning, engine check warning light etc.). Check for damage/corrosion at the dash fuse box.

NOTE:

- Before starting the applicable troubleshooting, check the condition of all SRS connectors and ground points.

- If the fault is not found after completing the applicable troubleshooting, substitute a known-good SRS unit and check whether the warning light indication goes away.






CAUTION: Disconnect both the negative and positive battery cables. Connect the short connector to the airbag assembly.
