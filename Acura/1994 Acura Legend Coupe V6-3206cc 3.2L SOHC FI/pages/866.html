California


Engine VIN Code I.D. [1] C32A1

Engine Displacement 3.2 Liter (3,206 cc or 196 cu.in.)

Engine Configuration SOHC V6


Market Certifications [2] 40, 41

Fuel System [3] PGM-FI

Computer System [3] PGM-FI

Ignition System [4] PGM-IG

Remarks:
[1] The engine designation (first five digits of engine number) is located on the top RH side of engine block.
[2] The emission certification (sixth and seventh digit of engine number) is located on the top RH side of engine block.
[3] Programmed sequential multi-point fuel injection.
[4] Programmed distributorless Ignition System.
