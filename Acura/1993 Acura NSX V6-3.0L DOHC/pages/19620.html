Brake Light Switch: Description and Operation

The brake switch signal is produced by the brake light switch mounted under the dash assembly. It sends a 12 volt signal to the ECM whenever the brake pedal is depressed. This signal is processed by the ECM to modify idle speed and charging voltages as necessary.
