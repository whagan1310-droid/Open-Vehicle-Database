Components

Fuel Filter:





PURPOSE
The fuel filter traps microscopic particles to prevent damage to fuel delivery and engine components.

OPERATION
Fuel supplied under pressure from the fuel pump passes through the filter element, trapping small contaminants.

CONSTRUCTION
The Fuel Filter uses a paper element to trap contaminants before they can reach the fuel injectors.
