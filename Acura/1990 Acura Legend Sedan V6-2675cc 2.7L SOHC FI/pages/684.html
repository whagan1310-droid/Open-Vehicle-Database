Fuel Delivery and Air Induction: Locations

Air Intake System:





AIR CLEANER
Located in the left front of engine compartment, next to battery.

AIR INTAKE TUBE
Located in the left front of the engine compartment, in front of the battery.

DASHPOT
Attached to the top of the throttle body assembly.

IDLE COMPENSATOR (FAST IDLE VALVE)
Located beneath the throttle body assembly.

RESONATOR
Attached to the bottom of the intake air hose.

THROTTLE BODY
Attached to the left side of the intake manifold.

THROTTLE CABLE
Located between the throttle body and the accelerator pedal.
