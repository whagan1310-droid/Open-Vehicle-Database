Brake Fluid Level Sensor/Switch: Description and Operation

With the ignition in ON (II) or START (III), battery voltage is supplied through fuse 13 and the brake system indicator light to the brake fluid level switch. If the brake fluid level is low, the brake fluid level switch closes and ground is provided to the circuit. The brake system indicator then comes ON to alert the driver. Check the brake system for brake pad wear and fluid leaks before adding brake fluid.
