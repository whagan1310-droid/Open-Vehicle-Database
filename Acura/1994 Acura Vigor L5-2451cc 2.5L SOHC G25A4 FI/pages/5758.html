Seat Belt Reminder Lamp: Description and Operation

With the ignition switch in ON (II) or START (III), voltage is applied to the seat belt reminder light. When you unbuckle the driver's seat belt, the integrated control unit senses ground at the BLU/RED wire. The seat belt reminder light will come ON and stay ON until the driver's seat belt is refastened. A beeper will also sound for about 5 seconds.
