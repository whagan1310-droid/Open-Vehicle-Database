Underhood Emissions Control Label Replacement (Supersedes 91-033, dated June 10, 1991)


BACKGROUND [NEW]

If hood replacement is necessary, it is not possible to remove an underhood Emissions Control Label from the old hood without damaging the label beyond use. A replacement label must be ordered.


ORDERING PROCEDURE

Refer to Parts Information Bulletin B92-0011, dated 9-15-92, filed under Procedure. The procedure for ordering labels has been changed:

^ Order on DCS, not from Acura Customer Relations.

^ Registration cards are no longer used; AH now has computer reports for records.

^ Labels must be supplied by the manufacturer for cars up to 10 years old.

^ If the vehicle is over 10 years old, contact your local Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and ask to speak to a state referee.

If you have any questions regarding this new procedure, contact Acura Customer Relations at (310) 738-3301 or (310) 783-3313.
