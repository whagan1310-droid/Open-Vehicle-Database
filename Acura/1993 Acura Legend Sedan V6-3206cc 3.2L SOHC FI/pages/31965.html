Headlamp Reminder Indicator: Description and Operation

Seat Belt Reminder


With the ignition switch in RUN or START battery voltage is applied to the seat belt reminder indicator light. When the seat belt is not buckled, the seat belt beeper/timer circuit in the integrated control unit senses ground through the closed driver's seat belt switch. The control unit turns on the reminder light and the beeper. The light and beeper will be turned off by the timer or by buckling the seat belt.


Ignition Key Reminder


Battery voltage is supplied at all times to the ignition key beeper circuit in the integrated control unit. When the ignition key switch is closed, the integrated control unit senses ground at G301. When the driver's door is opened, the door switch closes and the GRN/BLU wire is grounded. The beeper will sound until the door is closed and its switch opens.


Lights-on Reminder


When the headlight switch is in PARK or HEAD, battery voltage is supplied through fuse 49 to the lights-on reminder circuit in the integrated control unit. When the driver's door is opened, the door switch closes and the integrated control unit senses ground through the GRN/BLU wire. The control unit turns on the lights-on reminder chime. When the door is closed, its switch opens and the integrated control unit turns off the chime.
