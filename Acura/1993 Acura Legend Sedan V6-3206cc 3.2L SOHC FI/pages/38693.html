R-134A

NOTE:
The NSX R-134a expansion valve has inch threads, therefore requiring an identification label. The Legend expansion valve has metric threads with no label. Do not substitute R-12 parts for R-134a parts.

Reason for Change
The set pressure for the expansion valve was changed because R-134a has a higher operating pressure.
