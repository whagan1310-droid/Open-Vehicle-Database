DTC 3-12

Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) 3-12: Different Diameter Tire Diagnosis
The ABS control unit detects the wheel sensor signal speed during the regular diagnosis (during driving). This diagnosis is not performed when the parking brake switch signal is ON. The ABS control unit may turn the ABS indicator light on when one or more different diameter tires are installed.
