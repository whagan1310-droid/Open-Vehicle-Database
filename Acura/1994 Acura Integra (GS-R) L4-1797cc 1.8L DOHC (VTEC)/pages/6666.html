Shift Solenoid: Service and Repair




SHIFT CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE A/B
NOTE: Be sure to remove or replace the shift control solenoid valves A and B as an assembly.
1. Remove the shift control solenoid valve assembly protector.
2. Remove the mounting bolts and shift control solenoid valve assembly.
3. Check the shift control solenoid valve oil passages for dust or dirt, and replace as an assembly, if necessary.
4. Clean the mounting surface and oil passages of the shift control solenoid valve assembly, and install a new filter/gasket.
5. Check the connector for rust, dirt or oil, and reconnect it securely.
