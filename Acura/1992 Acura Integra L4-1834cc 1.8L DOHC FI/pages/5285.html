1. Disconnect battery ground cable.
2. Remove left and right side lower panels.
3. Remove center console assembly in number sequence shown in Fig. 5.
4. Remove left side knee bolster.
5. Disconnect instrument panel electrical connector at fuse box, Fig. 6.
6. Remove steering column retaining bolts, then lower steering column.
7. Disconnect ground cable at right of steering column.
8. Remove meter hood.
9. Remove four gauge assembly retaining screws, then pull assembly outward and disconnect speedometer and electrical connectors, Fig. 7.
10. Remove radio assembly retaining screws, then pull radio outward and disconnect electrical connectors and antenna cable.
11. Disconnect shift position switch and shift lock wire electrical connectors from instrument panel wire harness, Fig. 8.
12. Remove clock from top of instrument panel.
13. Remove left and right side defroster grilles.
14. Remove instrument panel retaining bolts, Fig. 9.
15. Lift instrument panel assembly upward and remove.
16. Reverse procedure to install.
