CORRECTTVE ACTION

1. Replace the Information Center Control Unit with a new unit.

2. Process the Repair Order as a normal full value replacement under Warranty.
NOTE: This bulletin terminates the Information Center Control Unit exchange program as of June 1, 1988.

3. Tag and store the defective unit as described in the Acura Service Operations Manual.
