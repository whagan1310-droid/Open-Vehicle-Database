Roof: Squeaks At the Headliner

ROOF

Squeaks at the Headliner

Symptom:
The headliner creaks when driving over rough roads.

Probable Cause:
Friction at two locations:

^ The fiber washer at the headliner, by the grab rail, is deformed.

^ The wire harness clamp at the sunroof rubs against the headliner.

Corrective Action:
Insulate the wire harness clamp area between the roof and the headliner.





1. Remove the fiber washer at the headliner.



2. Remove the fiber washers from the grab rail screws.

3. Remove the sunroof trim, door trim, and side trim up to the B-pillar.






4. Lower the headliner slightly and apply 3 mm EPT sealer as shown.



5. Reinstall all parts.
