Dash Board Removal and Installation





SRS wire harnesses are routed near the dashboard and steering column.

CAUTION:
- All SRS wiring harnesses are covered with yellow outer insulation.
- Before disconnecting any part of the SRS wire harness, install the short connectors.
- Replace the entire affected SRS harness assembly if it has an open circuit or damaged wiring.

DASHBOARD REPLACEMENT
1. To remove the dashboard, first remove the:
- Front seats
- Center console panel
- Center armrest
- Stereo radio/cassette
- Glove box lower panel
- Glove box
- L. glove box cover
- Dashboard lower cover
- Kick panel

NOTE (L and LS models): These versions have a stereo radio/cassette player with a 5-digit coded that protection circuit. Be sure to get the code number before:
- disconnecting the battery.
- removing the No. 56 (7.5 A) fuse. removing the radio.
After reconnecting power and turning the radio ON, the word "CODE" will be displayed. Then enter the code.


2. Lower the steering column.

WARNING: To avoid accidental deployment and possible injury always install the protective short connector on the inflator connector when the harness is disconnected.





NOTE:
- Remove the maintenance lid A, then remove the short connector (RED). Disconnect the connector between the airbag and cable reel, then connect the short connector to the airbag side of the connector.





- Connect the special tool to the cable reel side of the connector.





NOTE: To prevent damage to the steering column, wrap it with a shop towel.





3. Remove the nuts and screw, then remove the passenger's airbag brackets (L/LS model).





WARNING: To avoid accidental deployment and possible injury always install the protective short connector on the inflator connector when the harness is disconnected.

NOTE:
- Disconnect the connector between the passenger's airbag and SRS main wire harness. Connect the short connector (RED) to the airbag side of the connector.





- Connect the special tool to the SRS main wire harness side of the connector.

4. Remove the glove box side cover and dashboard side cover.
5. Disconnect the connectors.
6. Disconnect the opener cable from the hood release handle.













7. Remove the 6 mounting bolts, then lift and remove the dashboard.

NOTE:
- Use protective tape on the bottom of the front pillar trim.
- Take care not to scratch the dashboard.
- When prying with a flat tip screwdriver, wrap it with protective tape to prevent damage.

8. Installation is the reverse of the removal procedure.

NOTE:
- Make sure the dashboard fits onto the guide pin correctly.
- Before tightening the dashboard bolts, make sure the dashboard wires are not pinched.
