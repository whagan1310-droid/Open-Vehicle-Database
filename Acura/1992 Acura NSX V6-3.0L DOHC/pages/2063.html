DTC 5











Self-diagnosis Check Engine light indicates code 5: A mechanical problem (broken hose) in the Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP) Sensor System.
The MAP sensor converts manifold absolute pressure into electrical signals and inputs the ECU.
