Ball Joint: Service and Repair

Ball Joint Replacement


1. Remove steering knuckle as described under Steering and Suspension / Steering / Steering Knuckle / Service and Repair.
2. Remove ball joint boot snap ring and the boot.
3. Remove circlip, then press ball joint out of steering knuckle using a suitable tool.
4. Reverse procedure to install.
