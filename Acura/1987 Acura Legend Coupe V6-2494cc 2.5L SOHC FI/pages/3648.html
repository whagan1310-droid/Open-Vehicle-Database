Clutch Master Cylinder: Service and Repair


1. Working from underneath dash panel, pry out cotter pin, then remove clutch pedal pivot pin from yoke.
2. Remove attaching nuts at dash panel, disconnect hoses, then remove master cylinder from vehicle. Plug hoses to prevent fluid loss.
3. Reverse procedure to install, torquing attaching nuts to 16 ft. lbs.
4. Adjust clutch as outlined previously.
