DTC 13

DTC 12 - BAROMETRIC PRESSURE (BARO SENSOR)




PART 1 OF 2




PART 2 OF 2

The Malfunction Indicator Lamp (MIL) indicates Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) 13: A problem in the Barometric Pressure (BARO) Sensor.

The BARO Sensor is built into the ECM (Engine Control Module) or PCM (Powertrain Control Module).
