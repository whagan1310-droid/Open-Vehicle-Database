1995 Acura Paint Codes
(Supersedes 94-014, dated October 25, 1994)








Paint formulations are determined by each paint company. For questions about formulas or matching, contact your local paint distributor or the paint company's nearest regional office. The information provided is for reference only. American Honda does not endorse any paint company or type of paint.

The original paint is acrylic enamel. Paint codes with "M" are metallic colors; those with "P" are pearlescent colors.

NOTE:
Herberts Standox uses the Acura Paint Code as its paint intermix code.
