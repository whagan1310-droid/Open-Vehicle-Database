Except Retractable Headlights

! WARNING

You could Injure someone's fingers by pinching them between the headlight covers and body panels as the headlights are raised or lowered. Before you turn the headlights ON or OFF, make sure no one Is near them.


Battery voltage is supplied to the headlight relay at all times.


Low Beam Operation

With the combination light switch in HEAD, the headlight relay coil is grounded at G401. The energized relay supplies battery voltage to the dimmer relay coil, through fuse 52 and the RED/YEL wire, and through fuse 49 and the RED/GRN wire to the left and right headlight assemblies and the high beam indicator in the gauge assembly. The low beams then come on. The low beam bulbs are grounded at G403.


High Beam Operation

With the combination light switch in HEAD, the headlight relay coil is grounded at G401. The energized relay supplies battery voltage to the dimmer relay coil and through fuse 52 and the RED/YEL wire and fuse 49 and the RED/GRN wire to the left and right headlight assemblies and the high beam indicator in the gauge assembly. With the dimmer circuit (in the turn signal/dimmer switch) in HI, the dimmer relay is energized. The high beams and indicator bulb then come on along with the low beam headlights. (See low beam operation.) The high beams and high beam indicator are grounded through the RED/BLU wire and the energized dimmer relay to G201.


Flash Operation

The flash feature works with the combination switch in OFF, PARK or HEAD (low beams). When you pull the switch lever, the flash-to-pass circuit in the turn signal/dimmer switch moves to flash, ground is provided to the headlight relay, the dimmer relay, and to the flash/security timed OPEN request input at the retractable headlights control unit. The headlights will open for several seconds (if they are not already open) and both the high and low beams will come on. The headlights will close several seconds after you release the lever.
