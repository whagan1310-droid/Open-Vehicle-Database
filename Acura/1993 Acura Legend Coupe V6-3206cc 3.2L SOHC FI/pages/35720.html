Transmission Shift Position Indicator Lamp: Description and Operation

When the ignition is in RUN or START battery voltage is supplied to the A/T gear position dimmer in the gauge assembly. The dimmer supplies battery voltage to the indicator bulbs. When the headlight switch is in HEAD or PARK, battery voltage is applied to the dimmer through fuse 49, and the indicator dims according to the dim level requested through the RED wire. When the transmission is in park, reverse, neutral, D3, 2, or 1, the A/T gear position switch grounds the appropriate bulb, and the bulb comes on. When the transmission is in D4, the automatic transmission control unit controls the indicator by applying a signal to the YEL/RED wire to turn on the transistor. The transistor connects the D4 indicator to ground and the bulb comes on.


The D4 indicator is used to display the automatic transmission control unit trouble codes. When commanded to display codes, the control unit sends a signal through the YEL/GRN wire to turn on the transistor. The transistor grounds the "dimming cancel" input of the dimmer; the control unit then displays codes by turning the D4 indicator on and off at full brightness.
