Dashpot: Description and Operation

Throttle Control System:





The Dashpot is located on the throttle body, it's function is to slightly open the throttle valve during cranking to assist in engine starting. When the engine has started, engine intake manifold vacuum is applied to the dashpot diaphragm, causing the diaphragm rod to return the throttle valve to the normal idle position.
