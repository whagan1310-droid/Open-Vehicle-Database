How to use this picker

Click the screenshots in steps 3–5 to open the full PNG in a new tab (native pixel size; use the browser zoom if you want it larger on screen).

  1. Make your selection below using Make, Year, Model, and Engine.
  2. At the bottom of this page, the Manual link is your best match. It opens Operation CHARM on charm.li. Whether you land in Repair and Diagnosis or Parts and Labor depends on the CHARM menu section you chose above.
  3. On the charm.li manual page, click Expand All (for easy ctrl+f) first. If anything is still collapsed, click it again until the full outline is open.
  4. Press Ctrl+F on Windows or Linux, or Cmd+F on Mac. That opens your browser’s find box so you can search all expanded text on the page.
  5. Click the image above to enlarge it (full tab). In that larger view you can see your search text in the find box, matches highlighted in the outline, and the match counter (for example 1/9 — first of nine matches), as in this detail: The same expand-then-search steps work on both Repair and Diagnosis and Parts and Labor.

For remote charm.li manuals, the section choice updates the link below. Local exports open your files as usual; this control is disabled for those vehicles.
