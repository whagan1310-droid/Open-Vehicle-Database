Door Locks: Description and Operation

Battery voltage is supplied at all times through fuse 35 to the power door lock control unit. The control unit is grounded at G401.

The power door lock control unit is designed to prevent the accidental locking of keys in the car with the key in the ignition. When the ignition key switch is closed (key in ignition), the control unit will not allow the doors to be locked if one or both of the door switches are closed (door open).

When you press the driver's or passenger's door lock switch to either LOCK or UNLOCK, the LOCK or UNLOCK input terminal of the control unit is grounded. The control unit then applies voltage to the motors in the driver's and passenger's door lock actuators, and both doors lock or unlock.

When the key is used to lock or unlock either the driver's or passenger's door, the key switch in the door lock actuator grounds the LOCK or UNLOCK input terminal on the power door lock control unit. The control unit then applies voltage to the motors in the driver's and passenger's door lock actuators and both doors lock or unlock.

When you use the knob to lock or unlock the driver's door, the knob switch in the driver's door lock actuator grounds the LOCK or UNLOCK input terminal on the power door lock control unit. The control unit then applies voltage to the motors in the driver's and passenger's door lock actuators, and both doors lock or unlock.


The passenger's door lock knob locks and unlocks only the passenger's door.
