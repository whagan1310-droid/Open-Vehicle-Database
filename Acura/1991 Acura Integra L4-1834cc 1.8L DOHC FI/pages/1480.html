A/C Refrigerant Oil Replacement

When replacing the compressor, drain 1 ounce of oil from replacement compressor prior to installation. when replacing other components, add oil as follows:
Condenser:
Integra 1/3 ounce
1989-90 Legend 1 ounce
1991-92 Legend 1/2 ounce
1992 Vigor 2/3 ounce
Evaporator:
Integra 1 ounce
1989 Legend 2 ounces
1991-92 Legend 1/2 ounce
1992 Vigor 1/2 ounce
Lines 1/3 ounce
Receiver 1/3 ounce
