Marker Lamp: Description and Operation

When you move the combination light switch to PARK or HEAD, the taillight relay is energized. The relay contacts close, supplying battery voltage through fuse 38 and the RED/BLK wire to the side marker flasher circuit in the integrated control unit. The front side marker lights then come on. When you turn on the turn signals or hazard flasher, the left and right turn signal inputs to the control unit cause the side markers to flash with each input signal. With the combination light switch OFF, the front side markers will flash at the same time as the turn signals. With the combination light switch in PARK or HEAD, the front side marker lights will flash in opposition to the turn signals.
