After Warm Up


SYMPTOM:
Frequent Stalling After Warming Up
CHECK
1. Electronic Control Unit (*)
2. Electronic Air Control Valve (14)
3. Fuel Supply
4. Manifold Absolute Pressure Sensor (3) (5)
5. Vehicle Speed Sensor (17)

Numbers in parantheses are the blink codes displayed on back of the ECU. (*) LED on the ECU is not on, or stays on without blinking.
