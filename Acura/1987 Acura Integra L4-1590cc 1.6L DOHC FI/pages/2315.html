Rear

Fig. 1 Exploded view of rear suspension components. Integra:





1. Raise and support rear of vehicle and remove rear wheels.
2. Remove right and left caliper brackets, Fig. 1.
3. Remove stabilizer control arm.
4. Remove stabilizer assembly mounting bolt, then the right axle beam cap.
5. Lower stabilizer assembly from vehicle.
6. Reverse procedure to install.
