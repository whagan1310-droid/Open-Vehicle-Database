Ignition Coil: Description and Operation

PURPOSE
Provides a high voltage output suitable to fire the spark plug.

OPERATION
Increases the 12 volt primary signal to a high secondary output, through the collapse of a magnetic field.

CONSTRUCTION
A sealed module with an integrated sparkplug terminal, designed to be installed at each spark plug location.
