Cigarette Lighter: Description and Operation

Voltage is applied at all times through fuse 14 and fuse 22 to the cigarette lighter relay. When the contacts close, there is voltage available to the cigarette lighter. When you depress the lighter, the lighter element completes the circuit to ground. When the element becomes sufficiently heated, it is spring-released and the circuit opens.
