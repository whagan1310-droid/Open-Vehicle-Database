Cranking Signal: Description and Operation

PURPOSE
Sends a signal to inform of engine start.

OPERATION
The starter switch signal is generated by the ignition switch whenever it is in the start position. The 12 volt signal informs the Engine Control Module (ECM) the engine is being started. The ECM uses the starter signal for fuel and timing control at engine start-up.
