Wiper Motor: Service and Repair

1. Disable coded theft protection system as described under Accessories and Optional Equipment / Antitheft and Alarm Systems / Service and Repair.
2. Disarm airbag system as described under Air Bags and Seat Belts / Air Bags (Supplemental Restraint Systems) / Airbag System Disarming.
3. Remove cap nuts and wiper arms. Ensure wiper arms do not touch hood.
4. Using suitable tool, pry off trim clips and remove attaching screws for windshield lower molding, hood seal and air scoop.
5. Disconnect wiper motor electrical connector, then remove harness from wiper linkage.
6. Remove wiper linkage attaching bolts, then remove.
7. Remove wiper motor attaching nut and bolts, then remove.
8. Reverse procedure to install. Torque wiper motor attaching bolts to 8 ft. lbs, and nut to 16 ft. lbs.
9. Restore coded theft protection system and rearm airbag.
