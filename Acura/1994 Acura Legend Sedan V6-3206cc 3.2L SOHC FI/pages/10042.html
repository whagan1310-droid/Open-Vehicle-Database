Lamp Out Indicator: Description and Operation

The brake indicator system alerts the driver that the parking brake is applied, or that the brake fluid level is low. The circuit warns the driver by illuminating the brake system indicator light. The light is also activated by the brake check circuit.

Bulb Check
When you turn the ignition switch to START (III), the brake check circuit tests the brake system indicator light by grounding it. With the ignition switch in ON (II) or START (III), battery voltage is supplied through fuse 13 and the YEL wire to the gauge assembly. With the ignition switch in START (III), battery voltage is supplied through fuse 14 and the BLK/WHT wire to the gauge assembly.
