TCS Lights Do Not Come On











The TCS indicator light, TCS OFF indicator light, TCS activation light and the TCS switch indicator light do not come on for two seconds when the ignition switch is first turned on (no bulb check).

NOTE:
- The TCS indicator light normally stays on until the engine starts.
