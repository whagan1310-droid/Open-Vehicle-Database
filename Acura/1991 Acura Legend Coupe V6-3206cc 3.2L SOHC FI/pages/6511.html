RECALL: 1991 LEGEND A/T SHIFT CABLE


This Parts Information Bulletin supports Acura Service Bulletin 91-024 dated April 22, 1991. Please refer to that bulletin for complete technical details.

During a minor frontal collision, the engine and transmission rock forward on the engine mounts. This is normal. However, the transmission shift cable bracket may be damaged by this rocking even though there may be no visible damage to the car from the impact. If this bracket is damaged, the shift lever indicated position may not match the transmission gear position. The kit below must be installed in all affected vehicles.
