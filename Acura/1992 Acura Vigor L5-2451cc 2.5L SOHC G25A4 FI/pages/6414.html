Rattles At the Glove Box

Symptom:
The glove box door rattles when driving.

Probable Causes:
The instrument panel has been deformed by the rubber cushion on the glove box door. and the latch mechanism is vibrating.

Corrective Action:





1. Apply the 1 mm felt wool to the dashboard at the contact points between the rubber cushions and the instrument panel.



2. Remove the glove box lid trim.





3. Apply grease to the latch surface.
