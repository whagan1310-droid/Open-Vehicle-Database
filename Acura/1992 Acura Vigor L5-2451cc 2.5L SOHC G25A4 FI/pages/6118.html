Hood: Rattles At the Hood

HOOD

Rattles at the Hood

Symptom:
The hood rattles when driving over bumps.

Probable Cause:
Loose-fitting hood hinge pin(s).

Corrective Action:





1. Test drive the car, and determine which side of the hood is rattling.






2. Wedge a shop towel between the hinge plates, and test drive the car again.



3. Verify that the rattle is gone, and replace the hood hinge.

4. After replacing the hinge, check the alignment of the hood.
