75000 MI or 120000 KM

Check
    Valve Clearance
      Adjust as necessary.
    Brakes and Traction Control
      Inspect front and rear calipers, pads, discs, hoses, and lines.
Inspect
    Suspension
      Inspect suspension mounting bolts. Tighten as necessary.
    Steering
      Inspect steering operation, tie rod ends, steering gearbox and boots.
    Power Steering
    Parking Brake System
      Adjust as necessary. Should be fully engaged in 10 to 14 clicks.
    Exhaust System
    Brake Pad
      Inspect front brake pads.
    Alignment
Replace
    Oil Filter, Engine
    Engine Oil
    Coolant
      Check specific gravity for freezing point.
