Door/Trunk Ajar Indicator/Lamp: Description and Operation

The safety indicator lights come onto alert the driver to safety related conditions. The display consists of bulbs arranged to illuminate a graphic display of the car. With the ignition in RUN or START battery voltage is supplied through fuse 13 to the safety indicator circuit and each of the indicators.


Indicator Check


When you turn the ignition switch to RUN or START the safety indicator circuit grounds the indicator bulbs for several seconds. The indicators then come on for several seconds to test the display.


Ajar Indicators


When you open either door or the trunk lid, that door switch or the trunk latch switch closes, providing ground for the appropriate indicator. The indicator stays on as long as the switch is closed, and goes off when the switch opens (door or lid closes).
