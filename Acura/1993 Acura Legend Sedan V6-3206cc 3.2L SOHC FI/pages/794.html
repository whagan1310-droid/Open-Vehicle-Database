Fails Emission Test


SYMPTOM: Fails Emission Test

CHECK:
1. Catalytic Converter (TWC)
2. Evaporative Emission Controls (EVAP)
3. Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) System (12)
4. Manifold Absolute Pressure Sensor (3) (5)
5. Fuel Pressure regulator
6. Fuel Injector
7. Fuel Injector Resistor
8. Fuel Metering (43)(44)(45)(46)
9. Oxygen Sensor (1)(2)(41)(42)
10. Electronic Control Module (**)
11. Engine Coolant Temperature (ECT) Sensor (6)
12. Positive Crankcase Ventilation

Numbers in parentheses are the blink codes displayed at the MALFUNCTION INDICATOR LAMP. (**) MALFUNCTION INDICATOR LAMP is not on, or stays on without blinking. Notes on Reading Diagnostic List For ECM trouble code component testing procedures see COMPUTERS AND CONTROL SYSTEMS.
