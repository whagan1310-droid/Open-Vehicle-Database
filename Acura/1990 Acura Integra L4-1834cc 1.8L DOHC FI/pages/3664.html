Canister Purge Solenoid: Description and Operation

Purge Cut-Off System Operation:






The purge cut-off solenoid is located at the rear of the engine compartment. The solenoid is used by the ECU to control the evaporative emission system. The ECU applies voltage to the solenoid when engine coolant temperature is below 140°F (60°C). When energized the solenoid cuts vacuum to the purge control valve which prevents canister purging. For further information on evaporative controls refer to EMISSION CONTROLS.
