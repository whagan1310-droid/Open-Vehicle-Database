Compressor HVAC: Description and Operation









The compressor is a Nippondenso piston type. A revolving inclined disc drives the surrounding 10 reciprocating pistons. As the inclined disc revolves, it pushes the pistons, protected by a ceramic shoe, thus compressing the refrigerant.
