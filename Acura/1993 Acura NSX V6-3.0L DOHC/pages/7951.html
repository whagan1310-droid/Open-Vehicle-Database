Daytime Running Lamp Control Unit: Description and Operation

Battery voltage is supplied at all times through fuses 43 and 44, to the combination light switch and the daytime running lights control unit. With the ignition switch in RUN, battery voltage is supplied through fuse 3 to the daytime running lights control unit. The control unit is grounded at G401. With the ignition in RUN, the control unit will energize its relay when you release the parking brake. The energized relay then supplies battery voltage to the daytime running lights, and the lights come on. The lights are grounded at G301. Reapplying the parking brake will not turn the lights off until the ignition switch is cycled through the OFF position.

When you move the combination light switch to HEAD, battery voltage is applied to the headlights ON terminal of the daytime running lights control unit. The daytime running lights go off when the headlights are on. Turning the combination light switch to PARK or OFF will turn the daytime running lights on again.
