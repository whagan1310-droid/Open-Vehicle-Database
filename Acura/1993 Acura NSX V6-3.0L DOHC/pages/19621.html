Glove Box Lamp: Description and Operation

With the combination light switch in PARK or HEAD, battery voltage is supplied through fuse 38 to the dash and courtesy lights and the dash lights brightness control unit. The dash and console lights then come on.

The glove box, ashtray and cigarette lighter lights are not dimmed. (They are grounded at G401.)

The clock, stereo radio/cassette player and automatic climate control digital displays are dimmed to a preset level.

All other dash and console lights are dimmed. The dash lights brightness control unit controls the ground of these lights. The ground varies in response to the dimmer input from the dash lights brightness controller.
