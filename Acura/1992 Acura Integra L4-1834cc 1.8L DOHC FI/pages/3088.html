Customer Letter

June 1996

Warranty Extension: 1992 - 93 Integra Distributor Assembly

Dear Integra Owner:

American Honda has determined that the hot, humid climate and driving conditions in the southeastern part of the U.S. may affect the durability of the distributor bearing, a part in the Integra's ignition system.

Although you do not live in an area where the bearing's durability is an issue, Acura is extending the warranty on your car's distributor assembly to 6 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first, from the date your car was first sold. This is in the interests of customer satisfaction.

It is very unlikely that your car's distributor will ever develop a problem. But, if it does, your Acura dealer will replace it free of charge under this warranty extension.

If you have questions about this warranty extension, please call Acura Customer Relations at (800) 382-2238.

Sincerely,

AMERICAN HONDA MOTOR CO., INC
Acura Automobile Division
