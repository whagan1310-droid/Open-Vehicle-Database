1992 Acura Paint Codes








Paint formulations are determined by each paint company. For questions regarding formulas or matching, contact your local paint distributor or the paint company's nearest regional office. The information provided is for reference only. American Honda does not endorse any paint company or type of paint.

Herberts Standox and Spies Hecker use the Acura Paint Code as their paint intermix code.

The original paint is acrylic enamel. Acura paint codes which include "M" are metallic colors, those which include "P" are pearlescent colors.
