Fuel Supply Line: Testing and Inspection

Fuel Delivery System:





1. Inspect fuel lines for physical damage (i.e., cracks, dents, rust, etc.)
2. Inspect all connectors for signs of seepage.
3. Check and replace any cracked or swollen fuel hoses.
