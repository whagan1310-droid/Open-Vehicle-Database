Relieving Pressure

Fig. 62 System Air Bleeding:




Fig. 63 System Air Bleeding:





1. Drain brake fluid from master cylinder and modulator reservoir.
2. Remove red bleeder cap from power unit, Fig. 62 and 63.
3. Install ALB T-wrench, 07HAA-SG00100, on bleeder screw, about 90°, to collect high pressure fluid in reservoir. Turn T-wrench one complete turn to drain brake fluid.
4. Torque bleeder screw to 4 ft. lbs., discard fluid, then install red bleeder cap.
