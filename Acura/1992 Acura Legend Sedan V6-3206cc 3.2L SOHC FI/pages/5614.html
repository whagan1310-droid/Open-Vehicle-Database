Transmission Speed Sensor: Service and Repair






A/T Speed Sensor Replacement

1. Remove the 6 mm bolt from the transmission housing and remove the A/T speed sensor.
2. Replace the O-ring with a new one before reassembling the A/T speed sensor.

NOTE: Install the washer to the NC speed sensor before reassembling the NC speed sensor.
