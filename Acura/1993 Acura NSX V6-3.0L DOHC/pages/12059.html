Variable Induction Control Valve: Testing and Inspection

INSPECTION

CAUTION: Do not adjust the intake air bypass control valve full-close screw. It was preset at the factory.

1. Check the intake air bypass control valve shaft for binding or sticking.
2. Check the intake air bypass control valve for smooth movement.





3. Check that (A) of the intake air bypass control valve is in close contact with the stopper when the vacuum hose is disconnected from the intake air bypass control diaphragm.





4. Check that (B) of the intake air bypass control valve is in close contact with the full-close screw when you apply 380 mm Hg (15 in. Hg) of vacuum to the intake air bypass control diaphragm.
- If any fault is found, clean the linkage and shafts with carburetor cleaner.
- If the problem still exists after cleaning, disassemble the intake manifold and check the intake air bypass valve body assembly.
