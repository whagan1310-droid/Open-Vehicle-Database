Seat Belt Buckle Replacement

1. Remove both front seat belt buckle assemblies. Refer to section 20 of the appropriate service manual.

2. Install the new buckle assemblies. Refer to Parts Information Bulletin B95-0011 for part numbers and applications.





3. Center-punch a completion mark below the first character of the engine compartment VIN.
