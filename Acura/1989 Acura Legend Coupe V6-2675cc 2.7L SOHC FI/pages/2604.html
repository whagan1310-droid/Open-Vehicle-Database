DTC 6

Code 6 normally indicates a problem with the coolant temperature (TW) sensor. The ECU assumes the cooling system is functioning normally. If the engine will not reach normal operating temperature, (i.e. thermostat not closing), a code 6 may be set as a result.

1. Remove Alternator Sense fuse in underhood relay box for 10 seconds to reset ECU.
2. Turn ignition on and observe diagnostic LED.

Flashes code 6, proceed to step 4.
Does not flash, proceed to next step.

3. Malfunction is intermittent. Check connections between TW sensor and ECU. End of test.
4. Turn ignition off


Electronic Control Unit Pin And Connector Identification:






5. Install test harness between ECU and connector. If test harness is not available, carefully backprobe wires at ECU connector.
6. Start engine and run until cooling fan comes on.
7. Turn ignition off.
8. Disconnect connector C from ECU only. Leave connected to main harness.
9. Measure resistance between terminals C6 and C14.

200 - 400 ohms, proceed to step 11.
Not 200 - 400 ohms, proceed to next step.

10. Check for open in GRN/WHT or RED/WHT wires between ECU and TW sensor.
11. Reconnect connector C to ECU and remove from main harness.
12. Turn ignition on and check for voltage between ECU terminals C6 and C14.

5 volts, proceed to next step.
Not 5 volts, replace electronic control unit. End of test.

13. Turn ignition off and reconnect connector C to main harness.
14. Turn ignition on and check for voltage between terminals C6 and C14.

Less than 0.5 volts, A/T proceed to step 16. M/T proceed to step 17.
More than 0.5 volts, proceed to next step.

15. Replace electronic control unit.
16. Disconnect connector A from transmission control unit under drivers seat and recheck voltage across ECU terminals C6 and C14.

Less than 0.5 volts, proceed to step 17.
More than 0.5 volts, replace automatic transmission control unit. End of test.

17. Repair short in RED/WHT wire between ECU terminal C6 and TW sensor. End of test.
