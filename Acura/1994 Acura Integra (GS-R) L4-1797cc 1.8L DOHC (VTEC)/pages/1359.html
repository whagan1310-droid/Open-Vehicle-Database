Parking Brake System: Adjustments

After servicing rear brakes, depress brake pedal several times to set self-adjusting brakes before adjusting parking brake cable.

1. Block front wheels, then raise and support rear of vehicle.
2. Ensure rear caliper is in contact with pin.
3. Pull parking brake lever up one notch, then tighten equalizer adjusting nut, until rear wheels drag slightly when turned.
4. Release parking brake lever and ensure rear wheels do not drag when turned. When properly adjusted, rear brakes should be fully applied when parking lever has traveled 6-10 clicks.
