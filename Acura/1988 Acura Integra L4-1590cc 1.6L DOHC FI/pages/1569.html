Dash - Brightness Varies

88acura01

March 14, 1988
YEAR MODEL APPLICATION
1988 INTEGRA 5dr up to:
DA10003676 3dr up to:
DA30008294
88-007
Dash Brightness Varies


SYMPTOM With the dashlight brightness controller turned less than 1/2 intensity, the dash brightness may vary slightly with the engine speed.


CORRECTIVE ACTION Replace the dashlight brightness controller with the improved type listed under Parts Information. Refer to page 23-63 of the '88 Integra Service Manual for replacement procedure.


PARTS INFORMATION

Dashlight brightness controller: P/N 35150-SD2-902.


WARRANTY CLAIM INFORMATION

Operation number: 722140

Flat rate time: 0.2 hour

Failed part P/N: 35150-SD2-901

Defect code: 068

Contention code: B99
