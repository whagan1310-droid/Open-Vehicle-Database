Backup Lamp: Description and Operation

With the ignition in RUN or START battery voltage is applied from fuse 5 to the A/T gear position switch (automatic transmission) or back-up light switch (manual transmission). When you move the gear selector to reverse, battery voltage is supplied to the back-up lights, and the lights go on. The lights are grounded at G551.
