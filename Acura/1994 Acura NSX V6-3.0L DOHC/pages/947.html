Description of On-Board Diagnostics

Temporary Driving Conditions:

1. The TCS indicator light will come on and the control unit memorizes the Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) under certain temporary conditions:
- The spare tire is installed, or a tire of the improper size is installed.
- The tire pressures are not correct.
2. If the TCS indicator light does not come back on after correcting the tire or tire pressure problem, the TCS system is OK.

NOTE: Remove the ABS 2.3 (20 A) fuse for at least 3 seconds to clear the DTC from the TCS control unit memory.
