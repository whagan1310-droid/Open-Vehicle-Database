Intake Air Duct: Description and Operation

Air Intake System:





The Air Intake Tube is located at various positions in the engine compartment. Across the front of the engine, along the top of the radiator, along the right side of the engine compartment, and behind the top of the engine connecting to the throttle body. The Air Intake Tube routes fresh air to the intake manifold of the engine.
