Initial Inspection and Diagnostic Overview

SRS unit includes a self-diagnosis function. If a failure occurs in sensors, SRS unit, or their circuits, SRS light in instrument panel comes illuminates.

As a system check, SRS light also comes on when ignition is first turned to position II. If light goes out after approximately 6 seconds, system is satisfactory.

If SRS light remains on, or fails to come on in system check mode, an SRS component or wiring / connectors are faulty.
