Sunroof / Moonroof: Description and Operation

With the ignition switch in RUN or START, voltage is applied through fuse 16 to the coil of the moonroof relay. The moonroof relay energizes and voltage is applied from fuse 15 through the closed contacts of the moonroof relay to the moonroof switch.



Open Operation

When the moonroof switch is moved to the OPEN position, voltage is applied to the moonroof motor. The moonroof motor is grounded through the CLOSE contacts of the moonroof switch, and the motor runs to open the moonroof.



Close Operation

When the moonroof switch is moved to the CLOSE position, voltage is applied to the moonroof motor. The moonroof motor is grounded through the OPEN contacts of the moonroof switch, and the motor runs to close the moonroof.
