Fuel Tank Pressure Release Valve


IDLE UP SOLENOID
On below 1800 RPM and 10°C (14°F)

PURGE SOLENOID
On above 60°C (140°F)

TWO-WAY VALVE:
Open VACUUM 5 to 15 mmHg (.2 to .6 in.Hg)
Open PRESSURE 10 to 35 mmHg (0.4 to 1.4 in.Hg)
