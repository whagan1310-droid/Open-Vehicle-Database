Squeaks From the Grab Handle

Symptom:
The grab handle creaks when used.

Probable Cause:
Insufficient lubrication of the sliding surfaces.



Corrective Action:





1. Remove and disassemble the grab handle.






2. Apply grease to the sliding surfaces.



3. Reinstall the grab handle.

4. Test the grab handle for quiet operation and stability.
