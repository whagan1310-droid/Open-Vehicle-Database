Heater Core: Service and Repair

HEATER CORE REPLACEMENT
1 .Remove the heater-evaporator assembly. Refer to Evaporator Case.
Evaporator Case





2. Remove the mounting screw, then remove the air duct.





3 .Remove the mounting screw, then remove the pipe cover.





4. Remove the mounting screws, then remove the pipe clamp and pipe cover.





5. Unhook the clip from mode control motor rod, then remove its mounting screws and mode control motor.
6. Remove the mounting screw, then remove the evaporator sensor.





7. Remove the mode door arms, then remove its mounting screws and left side cover.





8. Remove the heater core cover, then pull out the heater core.
