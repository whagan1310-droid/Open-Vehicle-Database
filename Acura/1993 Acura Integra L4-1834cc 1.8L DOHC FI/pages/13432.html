Antenna: Description and Operation

With the ignition switch in ACC or RUN, voltage is applied through fuse 22 to the radio. Voltage is applied at all times through fuse 14 to the power antenna relay.

When you turn the radio on, the relay coil is energized and the contacts close. Voltage is applied to the power antenna motor and the motor moves the antenna completely up.

When you turn the radio off, the relay contacts open. This reverses the polarity of the voltage applied to the motor, and it moves the antenna completely down. A mechanical switch controlled by the motor turns the motor off when the antenna reaches maximum height or full retraction.
