Clock: Description and Operation

Battery voltage is supplied to the clock at all times through fuse 33 for memory and power. The clock is grounded at G401. When the ignition is in RUN or START battery voltage is supplied through fuse 5 to the clock, illuminating the clock display. When the ignition is not in RUN or START you can illuminate the display by pressing the display button. When you turn the combination light switch to PARK or HEAD, battery voltage is applied through the RED/BLK wire to the clock dim input terminal, and the display illumination dims.
