Fog/Driving Lamp: Description and Operation

Voltage is provided at all times through fuse 1 to fog light relay 1. With the dimmer switch in LOW, voltage is applied through fog light relay 1 to fog light relay 2. When the headlight switch is in HEAD and the fog light switch is closed, ground is provided to fog light relay 2. The relay contacts close, voltage is provided to the fog lights, and the fog lights go on.


When you flick the dimmer switch to HIGH, or turn on the fog light switch, ground is provided to fog light relay 1. The normally closed contacts of the relay open, removing voltage from fog light relay 2, and the fog lights do not come on.
