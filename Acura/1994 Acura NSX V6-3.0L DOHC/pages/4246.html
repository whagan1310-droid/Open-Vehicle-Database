DTC 10

The Malfunction Indicator Lamp indicates Diagnostic Trouble Code 10: A problem in the intake air temperature sensor circuit.








The intake air temperature sensor Sensor is a temperature dependant resistor (thermistor). The resistance of the thermistor decreases as the intake air temperature increases.
