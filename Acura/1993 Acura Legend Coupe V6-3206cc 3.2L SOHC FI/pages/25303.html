Ignition Coil: Specifications


IGNITION COIL RESISTANCE:
Primary [1] 0.9-1.1 ohms

IGNITION TIMING:
M/T [2] 15~ + / - 2~
A/T [3] 15~ + / - 2~

SPARK PLUGS [4]
Electrode Gap [4] 0.039 - 0.043 (1.0 - 1.1mm)

Capacity 0.47 + / - 0.09 microfarads.
