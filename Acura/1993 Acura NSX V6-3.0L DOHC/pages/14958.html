Antenna: Description and Operation

The power antenna extends for proper station reception when you turn the radio on. To keep it from being damaged, the antenna will retract when the radio is turned off or when the ignition is turned to OFF or START with the radio on.

Battery voltage is supplied at all times through fuse 34 to the power antenna motor. With the ignition in ACC or RUN, battery voltage is supplied through fuse 8 to the stereo radio/cassette player.


Extend Sequence

When you turn the radio on, battery voltage is supplied through the PNK/WHT wire to the power antenna motor. Battery voltage is supplied to the motor relay coil and the UP relay coil. The motor relay coil is grounded directly to G551. The UP relay is grounded through the limit switch and the motor relay (contact No. 1) to G551. The motor and UP relays are energized. The closed contacts of the UP relay supply battery voltage through fuse 34 to the motor, the motor is grounded through the DOWN relay (not energized) to G551, and the antenna mast moves up.

When the antenna mast is fully extended, the limit switch deenergizes the UP relay. Both windings of the motor are grounded and the motor stops. The diode between the DOWN relay coil and the limit switch prevents the DOWN relay from energizing.


Retract Sequence

When you turn the radio off, the motor relay deenergizes. Battery voltage is supplied from fuse 34 to the DOWN relay and through the motor relay (contact No. 2) and the limit switch to the DOWN relay coil. The coil is grounded through the motor relay (contact No. 4) and the UP relay (deenergized) to G551. The DOWN relay energizes, supplying battery voltage from fuse 34 to the motor, and the motor retracts the antenna. The motor is grounded through the UP relay (deenergized) to G551.

When the antenna mast is fully retracted, the limit switch deenergizes the DOWN relay. Both windings of the motor are grounded and the motor stops.
