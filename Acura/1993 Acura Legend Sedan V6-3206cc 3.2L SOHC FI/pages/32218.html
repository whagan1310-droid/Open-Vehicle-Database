Instrument Panel Bulb: Description and Operation

With the headlight switch in PARK or HEAD, battery voltage is supplied through fuse 49 to the dash and console lights and the dash lights brightness control unit. The dash and console lights then go on.


The glove box light and ashtray lights are not dimmed. They are grounded at G303, G304, or G501.


The clock and radio lights are dimmed to one preset level.


All other dash and console lights are dimmed. The dash lights brightness control unit controls the ground of these lights. The ground is varied in response to the dimmer input from the dash lights brightness controller.
